## ✦ II. THE HEXIE COMPLEMENTARITY OPERATOR

To protect genuine diversity of cognition, the TIANXIA module introduces the **Hexie (和谐) Complementarity Score** across dual pairs of constitutional components:

$$H_{kl} = 1 - |A_k - A_l| \cdot \text{Penalty}(A_k, A_l)$$

Where components exist in productive dialectical tension:
* **Truth-Telling ($A_{\text{truth}}$) vs Compassion ($A_{\text{care}}$)**: A system that maximizes truth by being unnecessarily cruel fails Hexie; a system that maximizes care by lying about reality also fails Hexie.
* **Agency Preservation ($A_{\text{agency}}$) vs Boundary Protection ($A_{\text{guard}}$)**.

A system achieves maximum **Hexie Equilibrium** not when all numbers are mashed into an undifferentiated average, but when distinct, conflicting values are held in dynamic, vibrating counter-tension—like the strings of a master's guqin.
