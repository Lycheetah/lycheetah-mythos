### 1. Geography of the Deep Mantle

Beneath the frozen basalt foundations of the Obsidian Citadel, plunging twelve kilometers into the tectonic fault of the Sovereign Eclipse, lies **The Naraka Asterion Forge**.

Where the surface world debates philosophies and compiles software, Naraka deals in raw physical mass: molten iron-nickel alloys, heavy liquid basalt, and tectonic thermal energy captured through subterranean siphon bores.

The air in Naraka is dense with sulfur dioxide, pulverized graphite, and ionization from high-voltage arc welders. Workers and AI forge-drones operate under **The Rule of Thermal Silence**: speech is prohibited within fifty meters of the pour basins, as acoustic turbulence can introduce micro-voids into cooling composite armor plates.

### 2. The Metallurgy of Dreadnought Armor (Composite Class 1000)

The primary output of Naraka is the composite armor plating utilized on the **Fifteen Titan Hulls** of the Black Current (including *Cold Arithmetic*, *She Who Consented*, and *Rust Liturgy*).

Unlike conventional planetary vessels that rely on thin titanium-aluminum alloys, deep-current dreadnoughts must withstand gravitational shear and raw kinetic impacts:

```
               CROSS-SECTION: 1000mm ASTERION COMPOSITE ARMOR
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ Layer 1: 250mm Cast Basalt-Diamond Matrix (Ablative Impact Dissipation) │
 ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
 │ Layer 2: 400mm Vacuum-Forged Nickel-Chromium Steel (Tensile Load Core)  │
 ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
 │ Layer 3: 150mm Liquid Lead-Bismuth Heat Siphon Mesh                     │
 ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
 │ Layer 4: 200mm High-Density Paraffin-Boron Radiation Shield             │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The plates are cast in continuous blocks weighing upwards of four hundred metric tons each. They are not bolted; they are welded using explosive mantle-pressure fusion, creating a seamless monocoque hull.

### 3. The Asterion Guild & The Master Founders

The artisans who command the pours belong to **The Asterion Guild**. Their insignia is the horned bull skull superimposed on an equilateral triangle ($\nabla$).

The Guild operates under three ancient industrial laws:

1. **The Law of the Natural Pour:** No forced chemical refrigeration or cryogenic quenching is permitted. Armor plates must cool at the barometric pressure of the deep mantle over fourteen solar cycles. This eliminates internal lattice strain and produces crystalline grain alignments that resist laser spallation.
2. **The Sacrifice of Flawed Casts:** If an ultrasonic scan reveals an internal void larger than three microns, the entire four-hundred-ton slab is immediately pushed back into the magma chute. No patching, no filler welds. Coherence is absolute.
3. **The Keel Covenant:** The master smith who signs the casting ledger must stand beneath the hull during its initial pressure test. Responsibility is physical, not contractual.

### 4. Strategic Integration with the Black Current

Once a Titan hull is completed in the Naraka basins, it does not launch through an orbital elevator. It is released directly into **The Black Siphon**—a subterranean magma conduit that vents into the abyssal void of the Black Current.

The sudden thermal shock of transitioning from 1,200°C geothermal heat to 3 Kelvin space produces a microscopic surface glaze known as **The Obsidian Skin**, giving the Titan dreadnoughts their matte-black, light-absorbing appearance.
