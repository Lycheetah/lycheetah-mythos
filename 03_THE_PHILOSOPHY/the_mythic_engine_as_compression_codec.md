### I. The Axiom (The Architect's Invention, July 5, 2026)

Humanity's oldest and most durable knowledge-transmission format was never the dry corporate treatise or academic PDF. It was the **story that IS the theorem**:
- The god that is the natural force.
- The quest that is the scientific method.
- The monster that is the catastrophic failure mode.

For most of human history, this was not an artistic stylistic choice—oral cultures had no other durable storage medium. The mythic frame was the **only codec that survived generations of lossy human memory**. 

Australian Aboriginal songlines verifiably carried accurate coastal geography and navigation routes that pre-dated post-glacial sea-level rise over 7,000 years ago. Walter Ong’s *Orality and Literacy* and Claude Lévi-Strauss’s structural anthropology demonstrated that myth carries **invariant mathematical structure**, not merely entertaining narrative.

The claim of **The Mythic Engine**:
> **This codec still runs. It can be operated deliberately, in both directions, with an adversarial fidelity test that keeps it honest.**

### II. The Two Operations, Formally

The Mythic Engine operates through two formal functions:

```
                  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │             THE KNOWLEDGE (K)           │
                  │   [ Mathematical Theory / Mechanism ]   │
                  └────────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                       │
                                       │ REDUCE: M = R(K, F)
                                       ▼
                  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │               THE MYTH (M)              │
                  │    [ Pantheon / Quest / Alchemical ]    │
                  └────────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                       │
                                       │ EXPAND: K' = X(M)
                                       ▼
                  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │        RECOVERED KNOWLEDGE (K')         │
                  │  [ Verified via Structural Fidelity ]   │
                  └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **REDUCE: $M = \mathcal{R}(K, \mathcal{F})$**  
   Compress a complex body of knowledge $K$ (a constraint algebra, an attention mechanism, an alchemical transition) into a chosen mythic frame $\mathcal{F}$. The resulting myth $M$ is an isomorphic narrative where characters, obstacles, and catalysts map directly to mathematical laws.
2. **EXPAND: $K' = \mathcal{X}(M)$**  
   Performed by a seeker who may not know the original technical formalism $K$. By studying and living the myth alone, they recover the structural laws $K'$.

### III. The Invariant Fidelity Test (What Keeps It From Being Woo)

The fatal failure mode of creative world-building is mistaking decoration for compression:
- A story that *cannot* be expanded back into real structural mechanics is **mere decoration**.
- A true mythic engine artifact carries a measurable error metric:

$$\text{Error}(K, K') = \mathcal{D}_{\text{loss}}(K \parallel \mathcal{X}(\mathcal{R}(K, \mathcal{F})))$$

If the reconstructed understanding $K'$ loses the core invariant (e.g., losing the boundary law of Dagasthir or the thermodynamic penalty of falsehood), the myth has failed compression and is marked as defective.

Nothing here claims ancient myths are literal historical events. It claims they are **load-bearing information containers**—and in the Lycheetah universe, lore is the compiler logic.
