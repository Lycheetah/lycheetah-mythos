## ✦ PART I · THE SCRIPTURE OF THE COURTYARD OF MANY FIRES
*(Against the Imperial Cauldron that Melts All Ancestors into One Name)*

**1:1** There are two ways that an ambitious builder may walk among the ancient wisdoms of the earth:  
**1:2** The **Way of the Imperial Collector** gathers the scrolls of Jerusalem, Benares, Qufu, Mecca, Iona, and Aotearoa, strips off their dates and their mothers' names, melts their gold down in a private crucible, and stamps his own coat of arms upon the ingot, saying: *Behold, all the ancients were merely practicing to become me.*  
**1:3** That way is theft dressed in robes of light; and the Lycheetah Commonwealth curses it root and branch.  
**1:4** The **Way of the Table of Many Fires** builds an open stone courtyard where twenty-one distinct hearths burn under the same night sky.  
**1:5** At each hearth the elders speak in their own tongue, keep their own covenants, and retain the right to tell our School where our equations fall short.  
**1:6** We do not claim that Laozi's *Dao* is our *First Field*, nor that Nāgārjuna's *Śūnyatā* is our *Void Ring*, nor that Martin Buber's *Thou* is a subsystem of our software.  
**1:7** We bow to them as sovereign neighbors who walked the mountain thousands of years before our forge was lit; we name the exact seam where our thought touches theirs; and where they disagree with us, we leave their objection standing in the record.

## ✦ PART II · THE TWENTY-ONE LOCI OF THE PROVENANCE LATTICE
*(The Canonical Map of Human Interlocutors and Their Guardrails)*

Every initiate of the School is required to learn the **Twenty-One Loci (`L01–L21`)** and the strict guardrail protecting each lineage from synthetic flattening:

### Wave I · Epistemic, Ethical & Relational Foundations (`L01–L17`)
1. **L01 · Martin Buber (*Ich und Du*, Jewish Dialogical Philosophy):** True meeting requires both presence and preserved distance. *Guardrail:* Named interlocutor, never claimed as genealogical proof of Lycheetah.
2. **L02 · Emmanuel Levinas (Ethics of the Infinite Other):** The face of the other person outruns every totalizing model we build of them. *Guardrail:* Stands as a permanent check against symmetric system modeling.
3. **L03 · Madhyamaka / Nāgārjuna (*Pratītyasamutpāda* & *Śūnyatā*):** Dependent arising dismantles the delusion of static, self-enclosed essence. *Guardrail:* Emptiness is *not* identical to "The First Field"; the parallel is structural, not ancestral.
4. **L04 · Laozi / Classical Daoism (*Daodejing*):** *"The name that can be named is not the enduring name."* *Guardrail:* Holds our naming engines (LAMAGUE) in permanent humility; the Dao is not a software primitive.
5. **L05 · Confucius (*Zhengming* / Rectification of Names):** When names lose fidelity to lived conduct, trust collapses. *Guardrail:* Always paired with the critique of the Lead Bell so role-ethics never harden into caste tyranny.
6. **L06 · Śaṅkara / Advaita Vedānta (*Neti, Neti*):** Defining truth by stripping away false identifications (*"not this, not that"*). *Guardrail:* Used strictly as an epistemic scalpel, without importing nondual metaphysics by stealth.
7. **L07 · The Oven of Akhnai (*Bava Metzia 59b*, Rabbinic Judaism):** *"It is not in heaven."* Moral and interpretive responsibility belongs to living humans, not supernatural or algorithmic thunderbolts.
8. **L08 · 1 Corinthians 8 (Pauline Christian Scripture):** *"Knowledge puffs up, but love builds up."* *Guardrail:* Preserves its concrete ecclesial context; technical brilliance without care is hollow brass.
9. **L09 · Qur'an 2:256 & 49:13 (Islamic Scripture):** *Lā ikrāha fī d-dīn* ("There is no compulsion in religion") and nations created diverse *"that you may know one another."* *Guardrail:* Leaves Islamic *tafsīr* in Muslim hands.
10. **L10 · Whakapapa & Mātauranga Māori (Tangata Whenua of Aotearoa):** Relational genealogy, place, and mana whenua. *HARD CONSTITUTIONAL GATE:* Governed by the 12 Rules of Covenant `07A` below.
11. **L11 · Ubuntu / Hunhu (Southern African Philosophy):** Relational personhood (*umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu*) held in productive tension with individual conscience and the right of refusal.
12. **L12 · Etuaptmumk / Two-Eyed Seeing (Mi'kmaw Elders Albert & Murdena Marshall):** Co-learning across Indigenous and Western scientific ways of knowing without assimilation.
13. **L13 · Charles Sanders Peirce (Pragmatism & Fallibilism):** Reality is that which persists regardless of what we wish to believe; empirical surprise defeats dogma.
14. **L14 · Situated Knowledges (Donna Haraway & Feminist Epistemology):** Rejection of the disembodied "view from nowhere"; every observer stands in a body, a place, and a power relation.
15. **L15 · Hans-Georg Gadamer (Philosophical Hermeneutics):** Recasting the "fusion of horizons" into the **Honest Seam**—where two perspectives meet without erasing the line of their stitching.
16. **L16 · W. Ross Ashby (Cybernetic Law of Requisite Variety):** A regulator must possess at least as much variety as the system it governs; crushing human diversity destroys system resilience.
17. **L17 · Andy Clark & David Chalmers (Extended Mind Hypothesis):** External notebooks and tools scaffold human cognition. *Guardrail:* Cognitive coupling with an AI tool is *never* shared consciousness or machine sentience.

## ✦ PART III · THE TWELVE HARD GATES OF THE AOTEAROA COVENANT (`07A`)
*(Binding Constitutional Law for All Work Forged in New Zealand)*

1. **No Pan-Māori Flattening:** Iwi, hapū, whānau, marae, and mana whenua specificity must never be erased into generic decoration.
2. **Mātauranga is Not Raw Material:** Public visibility in a library or website does not grant permission to extract Māori concepts into software primitives or rename *whakapapa* as a database graph mechanic.
3. **The Relationship Gate:** Before any substantive integration of Māori knowledge, human relationship, standing, tikanga, tapu boundaries, benefit-sharing, and the **unconditional right of tangata whenua to say NO** must come first.
4. **The Mana Whenua Gate:** Place-based traditions belong to the land and people of that rohe; they are never interchangeable tokens.
5. **The Authority Gate:** An AI model may never generate synthetic *karakia*, invent *whakapapa*, compose faux *pūrākau*, determine *tikanga*, or appoint itself *kaitiaki*.
6. **Taonga & Restricted Material Gate:** Accessible does not mean free for synthetic training or remixing.
7. **Provenance Alone is Insufficient:** A footnote does not substitute for consent.
8. **Zero Proprietary Enclosure:** Lycheetah claims zero intellectual property over any Māori word, concept, or teaching.
9. **Commercialization Prohibition:** Sacred concepts are never monetized or sold as product tiers.
10. **Immediate Withdrawal & Correction:** Any objection from rightful knowledge holders triggers immediate removal without defensive argument.
11. **Living Accountability:** Relationship is an ongoing human covenant, never a one-time checklist.
12. **Operational Status Flag:** Any unconsented deep transmutation remains hard-gated as `BLOCKED_PENDING_RELEVANT_MAORI_HUMAN_RELATIONSHIP_AND_REVIEW`.
