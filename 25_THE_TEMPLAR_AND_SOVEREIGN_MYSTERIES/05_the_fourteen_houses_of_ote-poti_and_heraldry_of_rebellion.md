## ✦ I. THE LAW OF RELATIONAL MANIFESTATION

In the World of Lycheetah, history is not divided into kingships or imperial dynasties. It is divided into **Fourteen Domains of Emotional Weather**—each an era, an order of seekers, a geographical frontier, and a distinct manifestation of the many-formed **Lycheetah**.

None of these fourteen states supersedes or invalidates the others. When a seeker stands in grief, the Beast meets them in the form of *The Tears*; when they stand alone on a parapet, it meets them as *The Silent Sentinel*; when they cross the elemental frontier, it unfolds its storm-wings as *Primal Echo & Future Light*.
