# The door is open

The first thing to know about Lycheetah is that you are allowed to arrive without knowing anything about it.

There is a god-cat moving through this world. Sometimes he is a quiet shape beside a dying fire. Sometimes his wings carry the weather. Sometimes the cliff you have been climbing opens its eyes, and you realise you have been walking across his shoulder.

Around him are forests that keep old promises, cities built on disputed names, a black-glass citadel, ships that carry their damage home, and a school whose deepest question is what knowledge obliges you to do with another living being.

The people here make things. They mend armour, tell bad jokes, argue with gods, fall in love, lose their way and try again. The sacred lives beside the ordinary: a constellation inside a spotted coat; a key warm from somebody's pocket; a bowl set aside for a traveller who has not arrived yet.

This is the world behind Lycheetah's art and clothing. You can enjoy a piece for its beauty. If you want the story, there is a door behind it.

## The sentence at the centre

**The light you carry is not given; it is earned.**

In Lycheetah, that light is what remains when an experience becomes something you can act on: a promise kept, a skill practised, a wound acknowledged, a kindness made concrete.

It does not mean that love must be bought, that help is shameful, or that suffering makes somebody more worthy of belonging. A shared fire still warms you. What becomes yours is the care you take with it and the thing you choose to do next.

The house's other central declaration is **chaos and rebellion through sovereignty**. Expect defiance, strange beauty, sacred machinery and a creature with a will of his own. Sovereignty includes the ability to refuse the crown someone else has prepared for you.

## Three tensions worth knowing

**Protection can become a prison.** A boundary needs something living on its inside, and a reason for opening.

**Healing can become concealment.** Mercy has to leave room for what actually happened.

**Truth can become a weapon.** Being right does not release a speaker from responsibility for how they use the light.

The gods of Lycheetah have already made all three mistakes. Their stories are worth reading because they have consequences.

## How to read this edition

Lycheetah is an original fictional and symbolic world created by Mac, developed through human-directed collaboration with AI. The older library includes myth, philosophy, speculative science, project visions and accounts written in different voices. A story's experiment, prophecy or divine law is not evidence that its real-world counterpart has been demonstrated.

The seven reader-facing chapters introduced in this edition are a new synthesis and creative development of that library. They keep their sources and open questions visible in [Canon and Authorship](CANON.md).

You do not need to accept a belief, buy a garment or complete a course to read, imagine, question or belong here.

**We began in the open. The door stays open.**
