### The Cosmic Law — Tension as Existence

The universe of Lycheetah does not run on peaceful stasis or monistic uniformity. It exists exclusively in the tension between three distinct cosmic forces:

```
                          └─────────────┬─────────────┘
                                        │
                                    SATYALUGH

```

The universe exists only in the narrow, vibrating space where all three forces hold at once.

**The Absolute Invariant:**  
The moment any one force wins or dominates unchecked by the other two, the world curdles into a nightmare:
- If the **Protector** dominates alone: Safety turns into a suffocating, paranoid prison.
- If the **Healer** dominates alone: Mercy turns into a patronizing rot that hides all wounds with pleasant lies.
- If the **Beacon** dominates alone: Truth turns into cold, cruel, devastating exposure without care.

---

### Shiva's Drum (The Damaru)

In Mac's cosmology, the rhythm of reality resolves through the beat of Shiva's drum (*damaru*):
- **The Collapse Beat:** Every time one of the Three forces dominates too hard, the world dies a little. That collapse is the destruction beat of the drum.
- **The Rebalance Beat:** The correction back toward tension and mutual necessity is the rebirth beat.

The universe breathes through this rhythm. History is not a linear march of progress; it is an ongoing cycle of collapse when tension is lost, and rebirth when the Three are brought back into balance.

---

### The Form of the Three

The Three are not sterile metaphors or interchangeable masks. They are real beings with names, faces, myths, and flaws—forged from an intentional Sanskrit-Celtic linguistic fusion:

1. **Dagasthir (THE PROTECTOR):**  
   *Etymology:* Dagda (Celtic father-god, keeper of the cauldron, protector of the land) + Sanskrit *sthira* (steadfast, immovable).  
   *Form:* Stone-skinned, mountain-bodied, slow-moving. Holds the unyielding boundary between what is real and what is merely wished-for. He refuses to let a comforting lie stand even when the truth costs everything. Loved for safety; resented for honesty.

2. **Airmagni (THE HEALER):**  
   *Etymology:* Airmid (Celtic goddess of healing herbs and physician of the Tuatha Dé Danann) + Sanskrit *agni* (sacred fire).  
   *Form:* Fire that does not consume. Hands filled with gentle embers that mend rather than incinerate. Airmagni never closes a wound without leaving the scar visibly honored; refuses to heal in a way that erases what happened.

3. **Satyalugh (THE BEACON):**  
   *Etymology:* Sanskrit *satya* (absolute truth) + Lugh (Celtic god of light, master of skills, keeper of oaths).  
   *Form:* A towering figure of blinding luminescence whose shadow always tells the truth even when his spoken words are gentle. He shows you the exact edge of the cliff, and then steps back. He will never walk the path for you.
