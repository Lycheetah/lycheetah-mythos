# The World of Lycheetah

*A reader's world bible · Public edition, 12 November 2026*

Lycheetah is a world of earned light: a many-formed god-cat, fallible divinities, strange companions and people making a life among the things that outlast their makers.

Its atmosphere holds Gothic stone, alchemical fire, wild weather, sacred geometry and the electric colour of a future that refuses to become sterile. A cathedral can contain a workshop. A machine can become a character. A creature's spots can open onto stars.

At its human scale, the world begins with a simple act: somebody finds a way through the dark and leaves that way open for another person.

## 1. The Southern Hearth

The Southern Hearth is the world's point of departure: the place where difficult work and ordinary welcome share a room.

Its origin imagery comes from rain-dark Dunedin and the southern sky. Cold air, wet streets, a light in an upstairs room, hands busy long after the rest of the town has gone quiet. The mythology takes that small, particular scene and gives it a horizon large enough for gods.

The Hearth's founding vow is preserved in the [creation myth](01_THE_MYTHOS/the_creation_myth_the_void_and_dunedin.md):

> “I will find the exit. And when I find it I will leave the door open.”

That vow gives the setting its direction. The world contains danger, secrecy and formidable thresholds, but its deepest welcome is available before a traveller has proved anything impressive.

The Hearth also makes the grand scale believable. Somebody has to mend the strap, keep a kettle warm and ask who is still outside. A light that matters to a galaxy should matter to the person standing in its doorway.

## 2. The many-formed god-cat

Lycheetah is a mythic hybrid with a recognisable feline ancestry and an existence larger than any single anatomy.

The cheetah gives him grace, alertness, speed and the long black tear-lines running from eye to mouth. His other forms may carry storm wings, cathedral antlers, a night sky beneath the skin, alchemical markings, a king-cheetah coat or the precise joints of a synthetic body.

A form belongs to a meeting. The creature who watches a solitary parapet need not look like the creature crossing an amphitheatre of neon weather. Neither appearance cancels the other.

This is the essential visual law of the world: **change of form reveals a relationship, a place or a question**. It is more interesting than a sequence of upgrades in which every earlier creature becomes obsolete.

In the oldest metaphysical account, the universe itself is Lycheetah's soul seen from outside. In the stories of the Threshold, he is an immense presence sleeping among broken gravity and inverted waterfalls. In clothing and emblem traditions, the same ancestry becomes something intimate enough to carry over the heart.

These scales belong together. The cosmic creature should still be capable of a quiet look across a room.

## 3. The Three who hold the world in tension

Three powers recur through Lycheetah's cosmology:

| Power | Gift | Danger when it acts alone |
|---|---|---|
| **Dagasthir, the Protector** | A boundary that holds | Safety closes into captivity |
| **Airmagni, the Healer** | The possibility of repair | Comfort hides the wound |
| **Satyalugh, the Beacon** | Illumination and witness | Exposure becomes cruelty |

They are characters with wills, habits and failures. Their disagreements move the world.

The [First Collapse](01_THE_MYTHOS/the_first_collapse.md) is the clearest example. Airmagni softens painful truths. Satyalugh reveals the concealment all at once. His revelation is accurate, and devastating. Dagasthir withdraws; without the boundary, healing spreads beyond its proper limits. The Three each find a reason to call themselves necessary, and together they discover how much harm necessity can do without restraint.

Brighvara steps into the wreckage and makes each face the others' necessity.

This is a durable story engine. A defender may be right about the threat and wrong about the prison they build. A healer may offer real relief while making an injustice harder to name. A witness may uncover a lie and still owe care to the people living inside its consequences.

The conflict has to remain alive in particular people and choices. A slogan cannot resolve it on their behalf.

## 4. Earned light

**The light you carry is not given; it is earned.**

The phrase is Lycheetah's central symbolic language. In the stories, light becomes visible when something difficult is made into a capacity: attention held, a promise kept, a craft learned, a break repaired, a truth carried without using it to diminish somebody else.

Its opposite is a borrowed appearance of mastery: an untested crown, an inherited answer repeated without understanding, a badge used to demand trust its wearer has not earned.

The distinction concerns responsibility. It does not make gifts worthless. A traveller can be fed by another person's fire, taught a skill, sheltered from a storm or loved before they can return the kindness. Those acts are among the reasons the Hearth exists.

Light is also allowed to be joyful. The world would be much poorer if every bright colour had to arrive with a certificate of suffering. Making music, finding a strange new form, laughing with a companion and building something beautiful are ways of bringing more life into the room.

The clothing carries the phrase as an invitation to meaning. It cannot certify a wearer's history or rank one person's life above another's.

## 5. Sovereignty and rebellion

**Chaos and rebellion through sovereignty.**

Sovereignty begins with a being having a voice in what happens to it. That applies to a traveller offered a bargain, a companion asked to obey, a community promised protection and a creature somebody has decided to name.

The setting becomes sharpest when a claim of care begins to sound like ownership. An institution may know the map of a forest and still have no right to flatten it. A god may answer a prayer and still have no claim on the rest of a person's life.

Lycheetah's rebellion has a constructive side. Someone opens another road, repairs an abandoned place, makes an original mark or finds a form that an official register has no space for. Defiance acquires weight through the thing that follows it.

The kingly imagery belongs here too. A crown can stand for self-possession and responsibility. When it becomes an excuse to make others kneel, it has entered the world's central conflict rather than escaped it.

## 6. A world with room for the wild

The [Lanternwild](REALMS.md#the-lanternwild) carries the scale of moss, roots and half-forgotten paths. The [Obsidian Citadel](REALMS.md#the-obsidian-citadel) gives conviction a dangerous architectural permanence. The [Black Current](REALMS.md#the-black-current) stretches into the weight and momentum of ships. The [Vale of Tamers](REALMS.md#the-vale-of-tamers) asks what companionship looks like when another creature can choose.

The [Cat God Threshold](REALMS.md#the-cat-god-threshold) is where the landscape itself becomes an encounter.

These places have different kinds of weather, work and risk. A forest journey should not feel like a fleet engagement with trees pasted over the stars. What connects them is the consequence of attention: what a character notices, what they overlook, what they keep and what they are willing to change.

The library contains several game and story continuities. This edition provides routes between their themes; it does not pretend that a single complete geographical map has already been settled.

## 7. How the sacred behaves

Lycheetah's sacred atmosphere comes from presence, recurrence and consequence.

A threshold means something because a crossing changes a relationship. A relic matters because it carries an act, a memory or an obligation. A name matters because somebody has lived with it and somebody else may try to take possession of it.

The world makes room for mystery that stays mysterious. A silent glyph need not resolve into a product feature. A sleeping god need not explain every part of the sky before a traveller can go home.

At the same time, wonder becomes stronger when the local scene is concrete. Tell us where the key is hanging. Let the cloth catch on the broken stone. Give the companion a reason to hesitate. Let a beautiful door stick in the rain.

Historical religions, alchemical traditions and philosophy have informed the older library. Lycheetah's beings and combinations are authored fiction. Their presence here does not turn new symbols into ancient artifacts or make different living traditions interchangeable.

## 8. The four recurring signatures

The [Chaos Lit account](01_THE_MYTHOS/the_chaos_lit_mascot.md) names four signatures of the creature's passage:

**Phoenix Forge Vector.** The capacity to make again after a structure fails. In a picture it can be a rising line, a flame finding its shape or wings beginning to open. Its story needs an act of rebuilding.

**Storm Walk Glitch.** The disturbance at a change of register. Something no longer fits the form it used to occupy. Its visual grammar includes interrupted contours, fractured light and movement crossing a boundary.

**Earned Light Chroma.** Colour that seems to emerge from within the creature or structure. It can be fierce, luminous and exuberant. It gives Chaos Lit its living weather.

**Solitude Engraving.** Deliberate stillness and a mark that can survive the quiet. A lone figure, a spare sigil, a dark field with one precise point of attention.

These signatures connect pieces without requiring the same image on every surface. A quiet emblem and a vast back composition can share a world while doing different work.

## 9. The collections are places within the whole

The collections are Lycheetah's different emotional weather: Pride, Tears, Shadows, Spellbound, Chaos Lit and the other chapters of the [collection atlas](COLLECTIONS.md).

They can become eras, orders, landscapes and manifestations in stories. They also remain actual creative identities with their own histories. Their meanings should stay distinct enough that a person can recognise why one piece belongs in Shadows and another in Chaos Lit.

There is no required order in which to encounter them. The person drawn to a cobalt guardian has not failed an entrance exam by passing the crimson furnace first. Someone may want the quiet Sentinel one day and the storm-winged creature the next.

Within a collection, each named work earns an individual story. Colour and size variants do not require invented extra mythologies. What matters is the particular creature, object or act the art makes visible.

## 10. The world leaves the door open

The mystery-school atmosphere offers study, company, beauty and discovery. Its promise has to survive an ordinary question asked by someone who has read none of the books.

A person can take pleasure in a garment without becoming a disciple. They can read a story without accepting its metaphysics. They can leave the room and return without being punished for the life they lived elsewhere.

Beyond the fiction, Mac's commitment to cheetah conservation gives the name a material responsibility. The pledge is **at least five percent of Lycheetah Clothing profits for his own Cheetah Life Preservation and Growth Fund, from the first sale**. Establishment, accounting and processed receipts are real-world work; a line of lore cannot stand in for them.

As of this edition, this is a founder pledge. This book does not announce an operational fund or report money processed. Any later public balance must be backed by actual processed funds and receipts.

The point is that the symbol should ask something of its makers too.
