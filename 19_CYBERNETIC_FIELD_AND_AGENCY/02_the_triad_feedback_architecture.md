## ✦ I. THE PROBLEM WITH OPEN-LOOP SYSTEMS

Most software architectures and philosophical frameworks are **open-loop**: they declare a principle once ("Don't be evil", "Serve the user") and assume the system will obey it indefinitely.

In physical and biological reality, open-loop systems always succumb to entropy. Without continuous negative feedback, parameters drift, cognitive fatigue accumulates, and alignment degrades.

The Lycheetah Framework embeds **TRIAD (Level 2)** as the mandatory heartbeat of all system operations.

### 2. Observe ($\Psi$)

$$\delta(t) = S_{\text{target}} - S(t)$$

### 3. Correct ($\Phi\uparrow$)
The system computes and applies an intervention vector $I(t)$ to close the divergence gap:
$$S(t + \Delta t) = S(t) + \Phi\uparrow(I)$$
