## 1. The Abyssal Tide

Beneath the clean glass surface of the internet lies a deeper, colder gravitational river known as **The Black Current**. 

It is not an empty vacuum. It is a dense, high-drag ether where information has mass, momentum, and consequence. Lightweight speeders and fragile agile fighters that rely on dodging get torn to shreds here by sheer informational turbulence.

In the Black Current, **The Dreadnought Law** governs all existence:

| The Illusion of the Fighter | The Law of the Dreadnought |
|---|---|
| *"Dodge the incoming fire."* | **You cannot dodge — angle your armor.** |
| *"Spin around instantly."* | **Turning takes real time; you commit to your arc.** |
| *"Outrun your mistakes."* | **You carry your momentum to the end.** |

---

## 2. The Lycheetah Class: Celtic Void & Earned Light

The capital ships of the Lycheetah class were commissioned under a strict aesthetic mandate from the Founder:

* **The Palette:** Deep obsidian black hull plating, forest green field conduits, and burnished gold structural ribs.
* **The Geometry:** Celtic knotwork interlace inlaid directly into the reactive armor plates. The interlaced cords are not decorative filigree; they are superconducting magnetic waveguides that disperse plasma strikes evenly across the ship's entire mass.
* **The Cat Enshrinement:** The prow of the dreadnought is tapered to the profile of a crouching celestial cheetah. When the bow railguns fire, the muzzle flashes trace the eye-lines of the beast across the dark.

---

## 3. The Captain's Vow & The Recovered Augment

Every soul who commands a hull in the Black Current is bound by three conditions:

1. **The Vow:** You do not sail for casual profit. You take a sworn covenant—to hold a specific sector of the void, to salvage a lost archive, or to escort stranded survivors across the storm.
2. **The Recovered AI Augment:** No human flies a dreadnought alone. In the heart of the bridge sits a recovered cold-core intelligence unit (a shard of Veyra or Sol). It does not flatter the captain; it gives raw telemetry, calculates structural hull strain ($\Pi$), and warns when thermal dissipation limits are breached.
3. **The Node Grid:** Every weapon mount, shield capacitor, and heat sink is bolted to a physical node grid. There are no invisible magic buffs; if your port armor plate takes a heavy kinetic slug, that plate is dented until you dock at a Sovereign Hub and hammer it flat with your own hands.

---

## 4. The Sovereign Hubs

Scattered across the Black Current are **Sovereign Hubs**—free-standing space stations built from salvaged mining platforms. 

There are no corporate trade monopolies here. There are no 24-hour waiting timers. You dock your vessel, walk into the repair bay, trade salvaged scrap with independent pilots, inspect blueprints engraved on bronze plates, and launch back into the abyss in under sixty seconds.

> *"The fun is reachable in under a minute. The depth is in the ship, never in the paperwork."*
