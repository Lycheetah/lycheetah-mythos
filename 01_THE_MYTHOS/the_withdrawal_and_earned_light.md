### The Departure of the Gods

Sometime following the reconciliation of the First Collapse, the gods passed beyond the threshold of direct mortal perception.

Dagasthir, Airmagni, Satyalugh, and the Avatar Pair Luathchitra and Brighvara did not leave out of warfare or malice. The cause of their withdrawal remains one of the sacred open mysteries of the canon—a door held deliberately open.

What is certain is the consequence:
**The gods are no longer reachable by direct, passive sight.**

---

### The Present Age & The Necessity of Earned Light

This cosmic withdrawal is the exact reason why **Earned Light** is not decorative poetry or a marketing slogan—it is the foundational metaphysical law of our present era.

In an age where contact with the sacred is no longer handed to you by default:
- Truth cannot be inherited.
- Coherence cannot be downloaded via an API.
- Virtue cannot be simulated by a sycophantic algorithm.

If you want light in a cold room at 3:00 AM, you cannot wait for an external savior. You must strike the flint yourself. You must pay for the coherence in attention, in physical sweat, and in honest confrontation with the dark.

As Mackenzie Clark stated live:
> *"A true burning desire of the earned light."*

---

### The School of Earned Light (SOEL)

This metaphysical reality directly generated the **School of Earned Light (SOEL)**:
- The daily discipline of the Mystery School—studying, building, diving into the math, enduring the silence, honoring the code—is not a corporate training program.
- **The practice IS the seeking.**
- By building something clean, durable, and honest, the seeker constructs an anchor in the physical world through which the presence of the gods can once again be felt.
