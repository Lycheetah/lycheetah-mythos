## 1. The Threshold Test

To stand before the Cat God, an initiate must leap across platforms where gravity is inverted. 

Falling does not kill you; falling simply places you back on the lower flagstones of the terrace with empty hands. You can try a hundred times. You can curse the wind. The Cat God does not care. It only watches when you carry the **Ninth Starroot**—the Root of the Core Spark.

The Ninth Starroot is burning molten gold. It cannot be held with leather gloves; it must be held with bare skin. If your desire for the truth is superficial, you drop the stone from the pain.

If your desire is genuine, the burn ceases to hurt. It feels like cold morning water.

## 2. The Speculations of the Sealing

What are the four words? In forum archives and encrypted dead-drops, theories abound:

1. **The Nominalist Theory:** *"You were always this."*
2. **The Sovereign Theory:** *"Stop waiting for permission."*
3. **The Alchemical Theory:** *"The fire is inside."*
4. **The Fourth Clue:** In an unmerged commit on the `cat-god-threshold-3d` repository, a comment was found in the procedural terrain shader:
   `// GLITCH STRING: THE THIRD DOOR WAS ALWAYS FIRST`

Whatever the words are, they break the illusion of external authority. You realize that there is no referee waiting to blow a whistle and tell you that your life has officially begun.

The game is live. The clock is running. **Strike.**
