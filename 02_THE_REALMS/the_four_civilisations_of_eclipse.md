## 1. The Axiom: Matter Has a Location

In lazy science fiction, items exist in an abstract inventory cloud. You craft a shield, and it magically appears on your ship.

In the Lycheetah universe, disembodied shortcuts are forbidden by cosmic law:

$$\text{exist} \longrightarrow \text{move} \longrightarrow \text{consume} \longrightarrow \text{fight} \longrightarrow \text{survive} \longrightarrow \text{break} \longrightarrow \text{rebuild} \longrightarrow \text{trade} \longrightarrow \text{remember}$$

If your dreadnought's port armor was sheared off in the Null Foundry of Erebos, you cannot buy a quick-repair kit from an in-game menu. You must physically limp across two sectors to **Asterion Forge**, wait for the gantry cranes to clamp your hull, and watch the sparks fly as local foundry-smiths weld fresh steel into the gap.

## 2. The Four Great Sectors

### I. Naraka · *"The Oldest Road"*
* **Cosmic Coordinates:** The cradle sector orbiting the dying Black Sun.
* **Key Monoliths:** *Asterion Forge*, *Aether Crucible*, *Cinder Relay*, *Pilgrim Halo*, *Black-Sun Threshold*.
* **Atmosphere:** Deep rust reds, heavy industrial smoke, massive smelting platforms anchored to hollowed-out iron asteroids. This is where civilization began before it learned how to build dreadnoughts.

### II. Erebos · *"Second Sky"*
* **Cosmic Coordinates:** The cold outer perimeter where spatial navigational reference begins to drift.
* **Key Monoliths:** *Helios Bastion*, *The Cold Cathedral*, *Mirror Well*, *The Bone Ring*, *Null Foundry*.
* **Atmosphere:** Deep indigo void, silent ice rings, ancient derelicts floating in zero-heat equilibrium. Sensor pings take forty seconds to bounce back.

### III. Aion · *"Gold Under Pressure"*
* **Cosmic Coordinates:** The radiant solar hub of sovereign governance.
* **Key Monoliths:** *Sundered Archive*, *The Gilded Crown*, *The Orison Vault*.
* **Atmosphere:** Burnished gold solar sails, crystalline orbital archives, contested royal lineages. Where the laws of the universe are written on bronze tablets.

### IV. Chaos Cradle · *"Broken Promise"*
* **Cosmic Coordinates:** The shredded borderlands beyond imperial control.
* **Key Monoliths:** *Wildgate Spindle*, *Carnival of Wrecks*, *Shard Spiral*, *Laughing Maw*.
* **Atmosphere:** A graveyard of ten thousand shattered vessels from three centuries of fleet wars. Independent scrappers, pirate outposts, and salvage fleets harvesting dark matter cores.
