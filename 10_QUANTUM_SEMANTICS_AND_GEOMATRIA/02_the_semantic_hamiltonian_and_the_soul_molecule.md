### II. The Semantic Hamiltonian ($\hat{H}$)

The total cognitive and energetic dynamic of an agent is governed by the **Semantic Hamiltonian**:

$$\hat{H} = \sum_i \omega_i \hat{A}_i^\dagger \hat{A}_i + \sum_{i < j} g_{ij} \hat{A}_i^\dagger \hat{A}_j^\dagger \hat{A}_j \hat{A}_i$$

Where:
- $\omega_i > 0$ is the intrinsic energy/tension of concept primitive $i$.
- $g_{ij}$ is the interaction coupling strength between concepts $i$ and $j$.
- The quadratic term $\hat{A}_i^\dagger \hat{A}_j^\dagger \hat{A}_j \hat{A}_i$ governs non-linear semantic interference (how holding belief $i$ alters the cost of expressing belief $j$).
