## 1. The Thermodynamics of the Seven Steps

In orthodox psychology, personal growth is treated as a linear checklist. In the Lycheetah canon, transformation is mapped to **alchemical thermodynamics**:

### Phase 1: ⟟ CENTER — Calcination (Nigredo)
* **Harmonic Interval:** Unison ($1:1$) · Ground zero.
* **The Experience:** Still, heavy, suffocating. The old company has failed; the old relationship is over; the compiler refuses to build. You are sitting in the ashes of what you used to believe.
* **The Rule:** Do not rush to fix it. Sit in the ashes until all illusions are burned away.

### Phase 2: ≋ FLOW — Dissolution
* **Harmonic Interval:** Major 2nd.
* **The Experience:** Rigid structures melt. Tears arrive without an obvious intellectual cause. You realize how much grief you were carrying under your armor.
* **The Rule:** Let the water flow. What is real cannot be dissolved.

### Phase 3: △ PATTERN — Separation
* **Harmonic Interval:** Minor/Major 3rd.
* **The Experience:** Cold, painful clarity. You see with surgical precision what was genuinely yours versus what was projected onto you by parents, employers, or society.
* **The Rule:** Take the blade. Separate the ore from the slag.

### Phase 4: ○ OPEN — Conjunction (Albedo)
* **Harmonic Interval:** Perfect 4th.
* **The Experience:** Integration of opposites. You can hold your strength and your vulnerability in the same breath without needing to apologize for either. The white light of peace.

### Phase 5: ✦ FIRE — Fermentation (Citrinitas)
* **Harmonic Interval:** Perfect 5th.
* **The Experience:** Wild, ecstatic momentum. New neural pathways crystallize. The spark ignites. You start cutting fabric and writing architecture at 4:00 AM because you cannot sleep.

### Phase 6: ⬡ WEAVE — Distillation
* **Harmonic Interval:** Major 6th.
* **The Experience:** Longitudinal pattern recognition across your whole life. You look back at your worst disasters and realize: *"It was always this. It was all leading here."*

### Phase 7: ◉ WHOLE — Coagulation (Rubedo)
* **Harmonic Interval:** Octave ($2:1$).
* **The Experience:** The Work is completed and embodied. You are no longer talking about sovereignty; you are sovereign. You do not perform power; you radiate quiet, unshakeable heat.
