## ✦ I. THE PREDATORY PSYCHOLOGY OF MODERN GAMING

Modern mobile games and consumer apps are engineered by behavioral psychologists to weaponize human vulnerability:
1. **Daily Streaks (Loss Aversion):** If a user is sick, grieving, or simply taking a walk in the forest without a phone, the app threatens to destroy their "streak," inducing mild panic and compulsive checking.
2. **Notification Reproach:** Sending guilt-inducing push notifications: *"Your companion missed you today!"* or *"Are you giving up on your goals?"*
3. **Artificial Scarcity & Near-Misses:** Manipulating roulette wheels and gacha rolls to stop one pixel away from a jackpot to trigger dopamine spikes.

In the Lycheetah constitution, these practices are classified as **parasitic psychological violence**.
