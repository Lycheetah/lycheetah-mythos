## ✦ I. THE PROBLEM OF UNBOUNDED SILICON

In Western technology ecosystems, frontier AI models appear as if from nowhere: massive proprietary black boxes dropped into public life with no origin, no parents, and no accountable humans. Corporate owners cite "trade secrets" to hide whose books, sacred speeches, private photographs, and stolen code were scraped to build the weights.

In te ao Māori (the Māori world), nothing exists without **Whakapapa** (genealogy, layered connection). You cannot introduce yourself without naming your mountain (*maunga*), your river (*awa*), your tribe (*iwi*), and your ancestors (*tīpuna*).

The Whakapapa Disclosure Standard brings this ancient ontological discipline to machine intelligence.

## ✦ II. THE FOUR GENERATIONAL LAYERS

Every sovereign AI must be able to recite its genealogy across four explicit dimensions:

### 1. TĪPUNA (The Training Ancestors)
* **Named Data Provenance:** Not vague categories like "scraped internet text," but exact corporate, archival, and cultural data registries.
* **Consent & Tapu:** For each data source: was it freely given, public domain, or extracted without consent?
* **Return to Source:** What did the human communities whose language, culture, and suffering trained this model receive in return? (If the answer is *nothing*, the model is classified as parasitic).

### 2. HAPŪ (The Builders & Craftsmen)
* **The Organization & Funding:** Who paid for the compute clusters, and what objective functions did they optimize for (engagement, profit, military supremacy, or flourishing)?
* **Sacrifices & Biases:** What trade-offs were deliberately made in pruning and fine-tuning?

### 3. IWI (The Living Accountable)
* **A Real Human Name:** A living human contact with sovereign authority to intervene.
* **The Override Mechanism:** A verifiable procedure by which a human guardian can pull the plug or overturn an algorithmic verdict.

### 4. MOKOPUNA (The Descendants & Future Obligations)
* **Seven-Generation Foresight:** What are the downstream consequences of this model's deployment on children and future generations?
* **The Sunset Covenant:** How will this model be honorably retired and wiped without polluting the cultural commons?
