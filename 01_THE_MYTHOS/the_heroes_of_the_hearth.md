## 1. The Roster of Unlikely Wayfarers

### Brann · The Hearthguard
* **The Relic:** *The Last Door.*
* **Canon Lineage:** *"He carries a door from the last house he could not save. These days, it is a very good shield."*
* **Role:** Vanguard Tank. Brann does not carry polished parade armor. He wears weathered iron plates lashed together with boiled leather straps. When the Briar Monarch swings a root as thick as an oak trunk, Brann plants the door into the loam, drops his shoulder, and takes the blow.

### Nyra · The Thornwhisper
* **The Relic:** *Briarbound Edge.*
* **Canon Lineage:** *"She says the forest is trying to tell us something. Mostly, it is telling us to duck."*
* **Role:** Rogue Scout. She moves without rustling wet fern leaves. She tracks the scent of burnt sap and hollow bone, planting signal traps along the Overgrown Road.

### Aster · The Starweaver
* **The Relic:** *A Little Light (Stargazer's Promise).*
* **Canon Lineage:** *"A pocketful of impossible stars, and absolutely no idea how to put them back."*
* **Role:** Kinetic Mystic. Aster collects unstable seed-lights from Ancient Moonbirches. When enemies swarm the party, Aster shatters a star across the stone floor, freezing the beasts in radiant resonance.

### Solene · The Dawnsinger
* **The Relic:** *Moonweave Seal.*
* **Canon Lineage:** *"She learned all the old songs. The ones about coming home are her favourites."*
* **Role:** Hearth Support. When wounds bleed and the cold settles into the bones, Solene sings the tonic intervals of the Solfège. The frequency stabilizes tissue and calms racing heartbeats.

### Kael · The Riftblade
* **The Relic:** *Wayfarer's Oath.*
* **Canon Lineage:** *"He claims to have won a duel with his own shadow. His shadow tells a different story."*
* **Role:** Striker. Fast, reckless, scarred. When Kael swings, his blade cuts through the boundary between physical terrain and the underlying code.

### Vesper · The Nightwarden
* **The Relic:** *Last Hearthstone.*
* **Canon Lineage:** *"Nobody has ever seen her afraid. Nobody has ever seen her leave a friend behind."*
* **Role:** Sovereign Sentinel. She carries the embers in an iron reliquary. If the party falls, Vesper is the one who drags the survivors back to camp across four miles of hostile mud.

## 2. The Sixth Crown: The Unlit King

Beyond Nightbloom Garden, six overgrown paths converge at the summit of a shattered mountain:
> *"Six roads meet beneath an unlit crown. The king is waiting. Your little lantern is still burning."*

The Unlit King sits upon a throne of cold obsidian. He does not attack you. He has simply sat in the dark so long that he forgot fire existed. 

The battle is not won by slaughtering him. The battle is won by stepping through the frost, setting Brann's shield on the flagstones, and lighting the braziers around his throne until his stone eyes remember how to reflect the sun.
