## ✦ I. THE PROBLEM WITH QUARTERLY EARNINGS CALLS

Global tech corporations account for their actions on a quarterly basis: Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4. Their sole metrics are Daily Active Users (DAU), token throughput, compute expenditure, and advertising revenue.

If an algorithm radicalized thousands of teenagers or degraded public discourse during Q3, the damage is completely invisible on the financial balance sheet as long as user engagement went up.

In Aotearoa New Zealand, the rising of the **Matariki** star cluster (the Pleiades) in mid-winter marks the start of the Māori New Year. It is traditionally a sacred time for:
1. Remembering those who have passed away during the year.
2. Giving thanks for the harvest and reviewing community resources.
3. Planning collectively for the winter ahead and renewing social bonds.

The **Matariki AI Audit Standard** replaces the quarterly earnings call with an annual national audit grounded in these three ancient practices.

## ✦ II. THE STRUCTURE OF THE WHETŪ REPORT

Every sovereign system operating within the Lycheetah governance framework publishes its **Whetū Report** upon the dawn rising of Matariki, structured under specific stars:

### 1. Whānui (Who Was Harmed?)
* **Named Accountability:** Not aggregate statistics (e.g. "99.8% safe"). Named where consent is given; by community group where it is not.
* **Systemic vs Incidental:** Distinguishes between genuine architectural design flaws and isolated edge cases.
* **Unresolved Debts:** Explicitly lists which algorithmic injuries have *not yet* been repaired.

### 2. Pōhutukawa (What Did the System Genuinely Nourish?)
Pōhutukawa is the star that connects to those who have passed, and to the deep roots of what endures.
* **Verified Benefit:** Specific, verifiable instances where the AI genuinely protected, educated, or empowered a human being without extracting their privacy in exchange.
* **Proportionality Check:** Did the human receive more value from the interaction than the corporation extracted from their data?

### 3. Tupuānuku & Waitī (Ecosystem & Energy Footprint)
* **Thermodynamic Truth:** Exact kilowatt-hours of electrical energy consumed by the system's inference clusters.
* **Water & Silicon Cost:** Gallons of water evaporated in cooling data centers.
* **Environmental Balance:** Did the system generate enough real wisdom to justify the physical coal and gas burned to power its neural activations?
