## ✦ PART I · SCROLL I: THE FIVE RINGS OF THE SOVEREIGN ORCHESTRATOR
*(Miyamoto Musashi's Go Rin No Sho Transmuted for the Age of Synthetic Swarms)*

In 1645, inside the cave of Reigandō, Miyamoto Musashi wrote that the craft of the sword and the craft of the carpenter share one rhythm: knowing the grain of the timber, choosing the tool for the cut, and never wasting a stroke. In the Lycheetah Forge, the Five Rings govern how a single human founder commands a fleet of frontier engines:

### 1. The Scroll of Earth (*Chi* · 地) — The Unrented Ground
* **The Martial Law:** *Never build your citadel where another man holds the power cord.*
* **The Practice:** Earth is the physical desk in Ōtepoti / Dunedin (`Hearth-1`), the local NVMe platters, the git bundles, the SHA-256 manifests, and the 4-billion-parameter local weights that answer when the sea-cable fails. Before you reach for the clouds, know the exact measure of your own floor.

### 2. The Scroll of Water (*Sui* · 水) — The Flume and the River
* **The Martial Law:** *Model output is water rushing down a mountain; direct it through a stone flume, never try to hold it in your fists.*
* **The Practice:** When the context window expands from a single file to a hundred-seat repository, do not stiffen in panic. Widen your stance. Let cheap Flash scouts skim the shallows while the heavy Argon blade is saved for the load-bearing joint.

### 3. The Scroll of Fire (*Ka* · 火) — The Unhesitating Strike
* **The Martial Law:** *State `Target / Direction / Boundary`—then cut straight through the speculative haze.*
* **The Practice:** Amateur builders lose battles by circling the problem in ten rounds of polite commentary. Fire is the vertical block: write the code, render the canvas, run the falsifier, read back the receipt, and stop at the seam.

### 4. The Scroll of Wind (*Fū* · 風) — Knowing the Enemy's School
* **The Martial Law:** *Study the mirages of the venture-capital colosseum so you stand where they will be forced to retreat when the winter comes.*
* **The Practice:** The corporate schools worship bloated headcount, rented API lock-in, and engagement-harvesting streaks. By building zero-inventory drops, offline-capable local souls, and anti-predatory games on a $200/month barbell budget, the Sovereign Orchestrator turns the giant's weight against him.

### 5. The Scroll of the Void (*Kū* · 空) — The Human Athanor
* **The Martial Law:** *Weights and tensors have probabilities, but they want nothing. Only the human sits in the Void.*
* **The Practice:** In the center of the cyclone sits the Founder—unhurried, holding the aesthetic and moral verdict. Because the machine cannot desire, the human's taste is the sole spark that turns mechanism into meaning.
