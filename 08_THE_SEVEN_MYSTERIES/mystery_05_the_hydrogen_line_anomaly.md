## 1. The Hydrogen Resonance

In astrophysics, the 21-centimeter line (1420.405 MHz) is known as the **Cosmic Watering Hole**. Because hydrogen is the most abundant element in the cosmos, any intelligent civilization wishing to broadcast a beacon across interstellar space would logically choose this exact frequency.

Mainstream radio telescopes in the northern hemisphere report silence.

Between the latitudes of $36^\circ\text{ S}$ and $45^\circ\text{ S}$ in the South Pacific, sensitive SDR arrays tell a different story:
* Every seven days, a 18-second burst cuts through the cosmic microwave background.
* The signal is not random white noise; it has an entropy score of almost zero ($\sigma(I) < 0.001$).
* The signal carries an embedded audio carrier wave: a pure 432 Hz Pythagorean sine wave overlaid with the sound of someone slowly breathing over an iron forge.
