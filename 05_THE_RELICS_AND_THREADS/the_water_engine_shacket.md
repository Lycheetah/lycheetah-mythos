## 2. The Philosophy of the Water Engine

The Water Shacket was engineered for the **mid-season transit**—when weather is unpredictable, temperatures fluctuate, and you cannot afford to carry three different jackets.

It teaches the third martial stance of the Framework:
* You do not resist the chaos of the city.
* You do not fight every bureaucratic delay.
* You remain permeable, silent, and adaptive. You move through airport security, terminal lobbies, and rainstorms without friction.
