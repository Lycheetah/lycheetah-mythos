## 1. The Physics of Truth Pressure ($\Pi$)

An idea is not true simply because a charismatic speaker said it with confidence. An idea is true only when its **Truth Pressure** ($\Pi$) can withstand the friction of the real world.

When someone invents a fantasy to protect their ego:
* They add excuses (*ad-hoc assumptions*).
* They ignore glaring inconsistencies (*structural strain $S$ skyrockets*).
* Because $S$ sits in the denominator, the Truth Pressure collapses toward zero.

When an idea has high Truth Pressure:
* It explains multiple disparate phenomena with a single elegant axiom ($P$ is high).
* It produces reproducible receipts that anyone can verify on disk ($E$ is high).
* The contradictions approach zero ($S$ is low).
