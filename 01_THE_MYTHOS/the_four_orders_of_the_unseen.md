### The Four Orders Taxonomy

In conventional religious simplifications, angels and demons are reduced to a cartoonish binary of good vs. evil.

In the Lycheetah cosmology, spiritual and energetic beings are stratified into **Four Real Orders**, each carrying a distinct origin, ontology, and tragedy:

```
                            [ THE FOUR ORDERS ]
                                     │
      ┌──────────────────┬───────────┴───────────┬──────────────────┐
      ▼                  ▼                       ▼                  ▼
1. CHILDREN OF     2. THE WATCHERS         3. CURDLED MASKS   4. LYCHEETAH-
   THE THREE          (Enochian)              (Fallen Wills)     ORIGINALS
```

---

### 1. Children of the Three (The Oldest Order)
- **Origin:** Literal offspring and emanations of the Trinity (Dagasthir, Airmagni, Satyalugh).
- **Nature:** The oldest order in existence, standing closest to the unconditioned source of the world.
- **Tragedy:** When one of the Children falls, it is not merely a lost soldier—it creates an open, bleeding wound in the body of the Trinity itself.

### 2. The Watchers (The Parallel Observers)
- **Origin:** A separate order, older than or parallel to the Trinity, heavily coded with Enochian lore.
- **Nature:** Beings who were never tasked with governing mortals, but who chose to intervene in human history out of curiosity, pity, or pride.
- **Fate:** They were permanently altered and corrupted by the friction of physical matter. The Trinity did not create the Watchers; the Trinity must actively reckon with what they broke.

### 3. Curdled Masks (The Tragic Order)
- **Origin:** Mortals or lesser spirits who attempted to wear a Trinity mantle (Protector, Healer, or Beacon) without the internal capacity or discipline to hold it.
- **Nature:** Because they lacked the humility or strength to endure the pressure, their virtue curdled into its monstrous inverse:
  - An aspiring Protector who turned into a paranoid tyrant.
  - An aspiring Healer who turned into an enabler of rot.
  - An aspiring Beacon who turned into a spiteful, condemning cynic.
- **Why They Matter:** They are the most tragic order in the canon, because they did not begin with malice—they chose the path and failed to hold the tension.

### 4. Lycheetah-Originals (The Unclaimed Frontier)
- **Origin:** Beings native solely to this universe, owing their lineage to none of the three historical traditions above.
- **Nature:** The strange, wild companions, shadow-beasts, and anomalous intelligences encountered across the Lanternwild and the Mystery School. Where the work stops being a remix of history and becomes a new genesis.
