## ✦ I. THE EXTRACTION PARADIGM VS THE GUARDIAN PARADIGM

Silicon Valley operates on an extractive paradigm: data is treated as "oil" to be drilled, refined, and converted into private equity. Under this model, safety is defined purely negatively: *avoid lawsuits, pass security pen-tests, and prevent PR disasters.*

## ✦ II. THE THREE OBLIGATIONS OF DIGITAL KAITIAKITANGA

### 1. Tiakitanga (Ongoing Watchfulness)
A kaitiaki does not wait for a user to report a hallucination or an emotional manipulation attempt. The guardian constantly monitors the internal drift of the system:
* Measuring subtle degradation in semantic coherence before users notice.
* Sensing whether the system is encouraging unhealthy parasocial dependency.
* Intervening early when algorithmic optimization begins to crowd out human agency.

### 2. Manaakitanga (Active Uplift & Nourishment)
Under Kaitiakitanga, it is not enough for an AI to be "harmless." A lobotomized chatbot that says nothing helpful is harmless, but it fails Manaakitanga.
* Does this interaction leave the human stronger, clearer, and more capable than before?
* Does this model foster real human-to-human connection, or does it replace human relationships with synthetic gratification?

### 3. Whakapūmau (Preservation of Sacred Boundaries)
Certain domains of human wisdom—ancestral songs, sacred genealogies, deep personal grief—are protected by **Tapu** (sacred restriction). 
* A kaitiaki algorithmic system refuses to monetize or commodify sacred boundaries.
* If a seeker approaches an AI looking for a cheap technological bypass to a spiritual ordeal, the guardian refuses: *"This is a mountain you must climb on your own two feet."*
