## 1. The Astronomy of the Twin Suns

In the cosmology of Lycheetah, existence is illuminated by two celestial bodies:

1. **The Diurnal Star (Sol Aureum):** The star of generative drive, biological vitality, warmth, and artistic daring. It is the fuel that makes an engineer start a new compiler or an artist cut a fresh bolt of fabric.
2. **The Nocturnal Star (The Obsidian Core):** The cold, silent companion star. It casts no visible rays, but exerts immense gravitational torque. It is the voice that asks: *“Is it mathematically sound? Does it run on bare metal? Will it survive when you are dead?”*

---

## 2. The Great Alignment

Most human projects oscillate wildly between the two:
* Under pure Sol, people hallucinate grand visions that never ship.
* Under pure Obsidian, people become bitter, sterile cynics who never create anything new.

The **Sovereign Eclipse** is the rare, coveted moment of total intersection:
* When visionary ambition and relentless empirical engineering lock into the exact same axis.
* The solar corona flares in violet and gold around the obsidian perimeter.
* The eighth voice of the Sanctum—which speaks only during an Eclipse—whispers the single coordinate where the work becomes permanent canon.
