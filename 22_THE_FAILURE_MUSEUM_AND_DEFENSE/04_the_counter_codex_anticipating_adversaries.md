## ✦ II. THE CORE OPPOSITION CASES

### 1. The "Theology Trap" (Unfalsifiable by Construction)
* **The Steelman Objection:** The nine layers of the framework compose into a system where any empirical failure in one layer is conveniently absorbed by another. If CASCADE's equations fail to predict a real-world event, the authors can claim "only the calibration failed, not the theory." If TRIAD fails a user study, the authors can say "the participants didn't anchor properly." This makes the framework unfalsifiable—closer to a self-sealing religious theology than an empirical science.
* **Our Honest Response:** Partially valid. As Thomas Kuhn observed, paradigms routinely protect themselves with auxiliary hypotheses. To prevent this, the framework established **Falsification Conditions with teeth**: If $k_1 - k_4$ fail on held-out historical datasets, CASCADE is declared dead—not qualified. 
* **Where We Concede Weakness:** The master differential equation ($\frac{d\Psi}{dt}$) contains four free parameters. In non-linear fitting, four parameters can fit an elephant. Cross-domain validation on completely blind data is required before claiming universality.

### 2. The "Alchemical Rhetoric" Objection
* **The Steelman Objection:** The framework uses classical alchemical vocabulary (*Athanor, Nigredo, Solve et Coagula, Chrysopoeia*) to create an aura of ancient mystical profundity. In reality, the mathematics is standard dynamical systems, and the lore is doing emotional heavy lifting that the equations haven't earned.
* **Our Honest Response:** The alchemical vocabulary is not decorative ornament; it is **pedagogical compression**. Alchemists spent two thousand years charting the qualitative phenomenology of transformation. Mapping modern non-linear phase transitions to classical alchemical operations honors the historical lineage of inquiry while giving human beings an intuitive, somatic language for cognitive change.
