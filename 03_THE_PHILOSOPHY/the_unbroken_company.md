## 1. The Shield Made from a Door

In the lore of Lanternwild, Brann the Hearthguard carries an impossible shield:
> *"He carries a door from the last house he could not save. These days, it is a very good shield."*

This is the central metaphor of the Unbroken Company:
* You take your worst grief, your deepest past failure, the house you could not protect in your youth—and you do not bury it in shame.
* You rip the door off its hinges, weld iron handles to the timber, and turn your grief into armor that protects your companions from the arrows of the dark.

---

## 2. The Law of the Hearth

At camp, the company abides by three unbreakable covenants:

1. **The Shared Broth:** The gold and ore mined from the Moonstone Quarry belong to the party, not the individual. If one hero goes hungry, the entire company’s strike damage drops.
2. **The Guarded Blind Spot:** In combat, your companion does not fight your battle; it guards your six while you drop the primary hammer strike.
3. **The Midnight Retrieval:** If a traveler’s nervous system is overwhelmed, if a coder spirals into despair, if a brother feels defeated by the world—we do not send a sanitized link to a corporate hotline. We pull up a chair, sit with them in the cold, and stoke the embers until the fire returns.

Sovereignty is not isolation. **Sovereignty is the capacity to be an unbreachable pillar for those who walk beside you.**
