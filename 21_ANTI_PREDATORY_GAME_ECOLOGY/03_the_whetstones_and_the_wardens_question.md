## ✦ I. THE REUNIFICATION OF COMBAT AND CONTEMPLATION

Most video games divide players into two rigid camps: fast-paced action titles that demand zero intellect, or text-heavy puzzle games that offer zero visceral excitement.

In The Architect's **Thinking World Specification**, the physical terrain of the game world becomes the living gateway to the Mystery School.

---

## ✦ II. THE WHETSTONES (Ϙ)

Scattered throughout the untamed lands stand ancient monoliths carved with the archaic glyph **Ϙ (Qoppa)**:
* **The Interaction:** Tapping the stone prompts `Ϙ PONDER`. The stone poses one philosophical or scientific riddle drawn directly from the Mystery School domain of that territory.
* **If Answered Correctly:** The stone grants mineral respect, a modest reward of light ($\diamondsuit$), and a permanent notation in the seeker’s ledger.
* **If Answered Incorrectly:** **Zero penalty.** The stone speaks the true teaching softly and points the seeker toward the corresponding door in the School.
* **The Rule of Peace:** The stone never mocks, never charges a toll, and allows only one inquiry per day (`DAY_SEED`).

---

## ✦ III. THE WARDEN'S QUESTION

In elite boss battles against the regional Wardens, combat is interrupted by a single dramatic beat:

When the Warden’s health bar drops past **50%**, the battle freezes for one suspended breath. The dust hangs in the air; the music lowers to a low cello drone.

The Warden looks across the arena and asks a single question testing the seeker's comprehension of the domain:

* **The Clear Eye:** If the player answers correctly, a surge of golden focus washes over the avatar. For the next fifteen seconds, critical strike chance spikes and enemy telegraphs slow down.
* **The Companion Clause:** If the player guesses incorrectly, **no health is deducted**. The Warden simply gives a nod of stoic respect for the attempt, and the fight resumes normally.

**Thinking is never punished.** An environment that punishes curiosity creates timid, anxious players. An environment that rewards curiosity creates sovereigns.
