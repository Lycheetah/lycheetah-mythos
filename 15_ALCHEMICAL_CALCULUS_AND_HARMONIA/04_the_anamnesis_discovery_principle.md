* **Ancient Egypt:** Inscribed in the perimeter-to-height proportions of the Giza monuments.

### 2. Pi ($\pi \approx 3.1415926...$)
* Archimedes in Syracuse (250 BCE) bounded it through inscribed 96-sided polygons.

* Madhava of Sangamagrama in India (1400 CE) derived the infinite series independently without European contact.

### 3. Abstract Symmetry Groups
