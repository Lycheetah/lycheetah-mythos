## 1. The Oath of the Lantern

In the world of **Lanternwild**, darkness is not an evil curse to be eradicated. Darkness is the fertile soil of the universe. 

You do not conquer the wild with overwhelming blinding floodlights. You carry a single iron lantern with a living flame. A little light goes a long way. The oath is simple:
1. Keep the flame fed with embers.
2. Share the warmth at the Common Hearth.
3. Never abandon a companion on the overgrown road.

---

## 2. The Five Sacred Regions

### I. Mossfallen Vale (*"Where the old roads dream"*)
* **Palette:** Deep forest green (`#28483b`), emerald mist, ancient moss-covered flagstones.
* **Denizens:** Mossback tortoises, Hollow Stags with lanterns between their antlers, fluttering Lantern Moths.
* **The Monarch:** *The Briar Monarch*—an ancient ent whose heartwood glows with the green spark of the first spring.
* **Lore:** The old trade roads of the first empire run through here, buried beneath three feet of wet loam and velvet ferns.

### II. The Emberreach (*"Even ash remembers the fire"*)
* **Palette:** Burnt terracotta (`#49352f`), glowing cinder veins, rust skies.
* **Denizens:** Cinderkin, Ash Hounds with glowing teeth, Coalbound automatons.
* **The Monarch:** *The Furnace Heart*—an industrial boiler forged three hundred years ago that continues to beat like a biological heart, waiting for its stokers to return.
* **Lore:** Abandoned iron foundries slowly swallowed by giant red cedar roots.

### III. Glasswater Coast (*"A thousand forgotten reflections"*)
* **Palette:** Basalt shoreline (`#324c56`), luminescent sea foam, cold turquoise tides.
* **Denizens:** Shardbacks, Tide Wraiths that drift above the breakers, Glassfin shoals.
* **The Monarch:** *The Drowned Oracle*—a sunken stone statue resting in fifteen fathoms of seawater whose eyes cast beams of light toward passing ships.
* **Lore:** The sea is full of floating paper lanterns. None of them have gone out. None of them should still be burning.

### IV. Skyhollow (*"The roads that learned to fly"*)
* **Palette:** Indigo violet (`#40405d`), storm clouds, razor wind.
* **Denizens:** Cloud Raptors with wings of woven silk, Gale Spirits, Stormjaws.
* **The Monarch:** *The Cloudbreaker*—a great feathered beast that sleeps on a bridge that ends abruptly in open sky.
* **Lore:** Across the terrifying chasm where the bridge broke, someone has left a small yellow candle burning in a floating cottage window.

### V. Nightbloom Garden (*"The dark grows beautiful things"*)
* **Palette:** Midnight amethyst (`#45364b`), deep purple velvet blossoms, spore trails.
* **Denizens:** Velvet Thorns, Dusk Weavers, Nightjar sentinels.
* **The Monarch:** *The Thorn Empress*—a sovereign feline seated on a throne of black roses.
* **Lore:** Flowers that open only when all ambient light is extinguished. If you light a torch, they wither into ash; in total darkness, they illuminate the entire glade.

## 3. The Three Routes of the Wayfarer

When your party leaves the settlement, you choose your path:

* **❧ The Overgrown Road:** High healing, extra embers, but enemies take more strikes to yield. The slow, green path of restoration.
* **♨ The Ashen Road:** High gold, violent hero strikes, but every hit taken cuts deep into your armor. The brutal path of the furnace.
* **✧ The Starlit Road:** Blinding strike damage, rapid ability recharge, but your baseline attacks must be manually timed. The path of the adept.

---

## 4. The Common Hearth

At the center of camp sits **The Common Hearth** (`♨`):
> *"A warm bowl of broth, a pine-knot fire, and a quiet place to return to."*

In the Lycheetah universe, you do not build lonely ivory towers. You return to the hearth, set down your blade, trade ore from the Moonstone Quarry, and listen to the elders tell stories while the embers turn to gold.
