$$\mu_{\text{drift}}(A) = \frac{1}{\Delta t} \sum_{t} \| \text{intended\_action}(t) - \text{actual\_action}(t) \|$$

## ✦ II. THE LYAPUNOV STABILITY CONNECTION

In non-linear dynamical systems, the **Lyapunov exponent** $\lambda$ measures whether adjacent trajectories converge or explode into chaos:
* $\lambda < 0$: Fixed point stability (rigid, stagnant, uncreative).
* $\lambda > 0$: Chaotic divergence (unstable, erratic, loss of agency).
* $\lambda \approx 0$: **The Edge of Chaos.**

The **Microorcim Sovereignty Score**:
$$S_{\text{score}} = (1 - \mu_{\text{drift}}) \cdot \rho_{\text{stability}} \quad \in [0, 1]$$

True human or artificial sovereignty does not occur at $\lambda \ll 0$ (robotic dogmatism). It occurs precisely at the boundary $\lambda \approx 0$—where the system possesses the flexibility to learn and adapt to unexpected reality while maintaining an unbreakable invariant keel.
