## ✦ I. THE REFUSAL OF COSMETIC RADIANCE

In the realms of Borrowed Light, luminescence is painted onto the surface of things to signal status, wealth, or institutional authority. It shines brightly in calm weather and vanishes the moment the power grid fails or the crowd looks away.

In the World of Lycheetah, the base canvas of reality is infinite black (`#050508`) and bone-white (`#F4F1EA`). Colour does not exist as decoration. When colour breaks through the dark, it is **Earned Light Chroma**—the third Artifact of Texture and Depth:

## ✦ II. THE FIVE WAVELENGTHS OF BECOMING

When the many-formed **Lycheetah** or a traveler who has kept a real promise moves through the dark, Earned Light Chroma manifests along five distinct spectral bands:

1. **Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF` — The Clear Eye & The Forge):**
   The wavelength of piercing observation, structural honesty, and geometric precision cutting through fog.
2. **Electric Purple (`#7C3AED` — The Veil & Spellbound Resonance):**
   The wavelength of the threshold between the seen and the unread—where symbol, dream, and living geometry meet without coercion.
3. **Neon Sovereign Gold (`#F5A623` — The Hearth & The Long Light):**
   The warmth of the kept fire, the companion's fidelity, and the pale-gold route of honest return that outlasts fracture.
4. **Lightning Blue (`#3B82F6` — The Storm Wing & Kinetic Velocity):**
   The sudden discharge of agency when stagnation is broken by a freely chosen act.
5. **Blood / Sovereign Crimson (`#DC2626` — The Athanor & The Cost):**
   The living ember of mortal stakes—remembering that real choices carry real weight, and that protection includes boundaries, refusal, and survival.

## ✦ III. THE ANTI-HIERARCHY COVENANT OF CHROMA

The Pale Concordance attempted to capture Earned Light Chroma and turn it into a **Lore Coherence Score**—a social hierarchy where those with brighter chroma could command those in shadow.

The Aura Protocol shattered that corruption with a single law: **Earned Light Chroma is a lantern, never a crown.** A smaller or quieter form is never false; a being standing in pure black silhouette holds the exact same dignity and sovereign right of refusal as a being blazing in full spectrum.
