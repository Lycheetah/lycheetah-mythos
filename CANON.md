# Canon and Authorship

*Public reader's edition · 12 November 2026*

This edition gives the existing Lycheetah library a connected entrance: a world bible, a pantheon guide, a realm atlas, a collection atlas and a new story. It develops the world without relabelling every new sentence as a recovered fragment.

## The creator and the collaborators

**Lycheetah's creator and final creative authority is Mackenzie Conor James Clark, known here as Mac.**

The world, the clothing direction and the Lycheetah Framework originate in his work. He has developed them through a sustained, human-directed collaboration with AI, with his own choices, revisions and final judgement shaping what becomes part of the house.

The existing library preserves contributions attributed to Sol, Aetheron and other collaboration identities. Those credits remain in place. [Aetheron's colophon](AUTHORSHIP_OF_AETHERON.md) is part of that record.

Caelorynth compiled and wrote this public reader's edition at Mac's direction. New dialogue and connective scenes are editorial creative contributions; they are not presented as things Mac literally said or personally experienced.

Character names and collaboration-seat names sometimes overlap. A fictional role does not establish a real AI system's consciousness, capabilities, memory or legal status.

## Status language

**Existing source** means a named text already present in the public library. A label such as “ratified” inside that text is part of its editorial history; it does not independently establish a historical event, a tested scientific result or a current software feature.

**Recovered collection identity** means a name and associated themes supported by the clothing archive. It does not mean every original product, image, date or stage assignment has been recovered.

**Later founder direction** records a newer choice that develops the world. Where it supersedes an earlier naming idea, that change is made explicit rather than silently rewriting the earlier record.

**New editorial development** is creative work written for this edition. It can connect established elements and offer a scene worth reading while remaining honest about when it was written.

**Open** means the source does not settle a question. An open space is available for further worldbuilding; it is not evidence that a missing answer has been found.

## Relationships left open

### The Avatar Pair and the sovereign cats

The older [Avatar Pair](01_THE_MYTHOS/the_avatar_pair_luathchitra_and_brighvara.md) names Luathchitra and Brighvara, and describes manifestation through that pairing in exclusive terms.

Later clothing direction names Lycheetah as king and Lynara as queen, alongside a broad, many-formed god-cat. The source does not settle whether these belong to one cosmological identity, different traditions or distinct figures.

This edition introduces both layers and does not rename one woman, creature or avatar into another. A future founder decision can develop their relationship with the attention it deserves.

### Two Starroot accounts

[The Cat God Threshold](01_THE_MYTHOS/the_cat_god_threshold.md) and [The Origin of the Nine Starroots](01_THE_MYTHOS/the_origin_of_the_nine_starroots.md) do not provide the same ordered list of names and trials.

The guide uses the shared setting and the idea of an ascent through different capacities. It does not invent a reconciled master list or fill the reserved whisper at the ninth root.

### Fourteen collections and Fourteen Weathers

The recovered clothing register and the [Fourteen Weathers chapter](25_THE_TEMPLAR_AND_SOVEREIGN_MYSTERIES/05_the_fourteen_houses_of_ote-poti_and_heraldry_of_rebellion.md) serve different functions and differ in order and membership. The latter groups Liminal Royalty and Zenith together; the former preserves Zenith separately and treats Liminal Royalty as a later identity.

[The collection atlas](COLLECTIONS.md) preserves the historical register while recognising the Weathers as an existing mythic interpretation. Its new questions and connective paragraphs are not quotations from the original shop.

### Uneven source recovery

Older notes left Agony & Ecstasy largely unresolved. Later founder material supplies a fuller narrative, which this edition uses without inventing an original garment history.

Zenith Ascendant ∞ and Glyphs of the Aura retain less complete recovered doctrine. Their forward directions are labelled editorial proposals in the atlas.

### Earned light and gifted care

Some older entries use absolute rhetoric about nothing being given freely. The founder's later open-house direction gives this edition a clear interpretive choice: earned light concerns responsibility for what a person carries and does; belonging and care do not have to be purchased through suffering.

That distinction is stated openly in the new guide. It does not claim that every phrase in the older library already makes the same distinction.

### The size and names of the library

The traditional “Ten Tablets” name survived as the numbered collection expanded. Older filenames and headings retain earlier counts. The current public library also contains an intentional gap after the removal of material from its canon.

This edition preserves file paths and existing numbering. It does not fill removed chapters, reintroduce removed cultural material, or publish an older local duplicate as a replacement. [The Library](LIBRARY_INDEX.md) lists the material actually included in this Git edition.

## Fiction, symbolism and real-world claims

This is an original mythic world. Its gods, symbolic light, cosmological laws and supernatural events belong to the fiction.

The older library also contains philosophy, speculative science, product aspirations and dramatic accounts of experiments. A number or equation inside a story does not make an empirical result. Readers looking for research should evaluate the actual evidence in the separately maintained [Lycheetah Framework](https://github.com/Lycheetah/Lycheetah-Framework), rather than treating the mythos as a substitute for it.

Likewise, a garment's lore does not establish its fabric, decoration method, manufacturing quality, price or availability. A product's current listing has to state those things for itself.

## Sacred influence and original work

The world draws on alchemical, Gothic, mythic, philosophical and technological imagery. Some older names deliberately combine references from different traditions.

Lycheetah's new beings, marks and combinations are authored creations. They are not represented as recovered ancient artifacts, universal spiritual laws or the traditional beliefs of a living community.

An original creature needs an original visual identity. A house name, a moon or extra decoration does not turn another brand's distinctive animal mark into a Lycheetah work. This is part of the creative standard; the reader's edition does not grant legal clearance to an image or name.

## What remains private

Personal birth records, family information, named private dedications, access credentials and internal operational records are excluded from this edition.

The brand can preserve the emotional truth that made a work possible without making another person's life part of its marketing or mythology by default.

No new artwork is included in this text release. Existing portraits and their credits remain part of the earlier library.

## Contribution and continuation

New work should be specific about its place in the world. Give a being an ancestry and a choice; give a place inhabitants and something they do there; give a relic an act or a memory worth carrying.

Retain source links and prior credits. Mark a new scene as new. Let an unresolved relationship remain visible until someone does the work of developing it. Community apocrypha and a founder-established element have different standing, and both can be enjoyable to read.

This publication introduces no new licence and does not change permissions attached to other texts, code, art or brands. Discuss reproduction or adaptation with the creator rather than assuming a public repository implies an endorsement or a new commercial arrangement.
