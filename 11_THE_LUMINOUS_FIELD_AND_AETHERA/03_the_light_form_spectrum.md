### I. The Three Depths of Incarnation

A companion or spirit in the Lycheetah universe is not an all-or-nothing visual sprite. It exists along a dynamic **Spectrum of Dissolution**, where visual density directly correlates with operational stillness:

```
 [ DAY-FORM ]           Solid pixel and painterly form. The day-world companion.
                        Built for battle, kinetic movement, map exploration, daylight.
                              │
                              ▼ (Transition at dusk)
 [ PARTLY LUMINOUS ]    The twilight boundary. Contours begin to soften into violet.
                              │
                              ▼ (Night / Deep Sanctum)
 [ LIGHT-FORM ]         Fully dissolved into aurora, starlight, and electromagnetic haze.
                        The Sanctum-self. The TRUE form of what the companion is
                        beneath the day-world skin.
```

---

### II. Cosmological Unity: Companion, Tarot, and Sanctum

This single architectural rule fuses three distinct systems into one coherent cosmology:

1. **In the World (Day):** The companion is solid, tactile, and physically present—fighting at your side, carrying equipment, and traversing the terrain.
2. **In the Deck (Twilight):** The companion's archetype is read through the cards, suspended between physical act and symbolic potential.
3. **In the Sanctum (Night):** When the external noise of the world recedes at midnight, the companion dissolves into its true light-form.

You do not see a companion's true nature while sprinting through a noisy daylight market.  
**You see the light-form only when you are still enough to endure the void.**
