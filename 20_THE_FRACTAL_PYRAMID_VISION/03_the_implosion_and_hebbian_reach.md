## ✦ I. WHAT HAPPENS WHEN AN OLD BELIEF DIES

In ordinary computer software, when a data node is invalidated, it is silently deleted: `DELETE FROM table WHERE id = 42;`. The user is notified with an error code, or worse, the screen updates without explanation.

In human life, when a core belief is shattered by reality, the experience is profound and destabilizing. You don't just "delete a record." You feel disorientation, grief, and fear.

The CASCADE engine was engineered by Mac to reflect **the somatic reality of cognitive implosion**.

---

## ✦ II. THE FOUR REGISTERS OF IMPLOSION

When an ancestral hollow-core block collapses under the weight of incoming counter-evidence, CASCADE executes four sequential experiential movements:

### 1. ⚫ Grief (Honest Acknowledgement)
The system does not sugarcoat the collapse with corporate optimism. It acknowledges what was lost: time, emotional investment, and pride. The collapsed block dims to charcoal gray.

### 2. 🕯️ Ceremony (A Marked Moment)
The transition is honored. The block is **never deleted**. It is preserved in the permanent archive as a **Held Implosion**. A lineage that does not remember its fallen hypotheses is doomed to repeat them.

### 3. 🎉 Celebration (The Self-Found Defect Rule)
The interface reminds the human: **This is your mind working correctly.**  
Finding your own defect before an adversary weaponizes it against you is the immune system of the soul functioning at peak health. You are not stupid for having held the belief; you are sovereign for having outgrown it.

### 4. 🌱 Hope (Structural Hebbian Reach)
This is the most radical visual mechanic in CASCADE:  
The child blocks that were supported by the fallen parent are not destroyed. They become **Orphaned Nodes**. In the UI, their latent connection axes begin visibly **reaching out across the screen**, searching for new, stronger baseline parents to anchor into.

## ✦ III. THE ATTUNEMENT PRINCIPLE
