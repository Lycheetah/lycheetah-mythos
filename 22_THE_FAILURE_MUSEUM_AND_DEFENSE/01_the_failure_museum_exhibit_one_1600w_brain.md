## ✦ II. EXHIBIT 1: THE 1,600-WATT BRAIN

### 1. What Was Claimed (March 2026, Session 8)
In an early draft of the Earned Light consciousness energy model (`ADVANCED_IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.md`), the framework included an absolute thermodynamic wattage calculation asserting that a human brain operating at peak sovereign consciousness could consume approximately **1,600 watts of electrical power**.

### 2. What Was Actually True
The human brain consumes approximately **20 watts**—roughly the power required to illuminate a dim LED or refrigerator bulb.  
**1,600 watts is the power consumption of a household space heater.**  
If a human brain consumed 1,600 watts, the blood in the cerebral cortex would boil, the meninges would liquefy, and the skull would cook itself from the inside out within forty seconds.

The figure had been derived from a purely theoretical mathematical extrapolation that failed basic biological reality checks.

### 3. What Changed
* All absolute wattage figures were immediately stripped from the framework.
* Replaced with a scale-invariant relative metric: $C(\Psi) = (\Psi / \Psi_{\text{baseline}})^2$ marked as `[SCAFFOLD]`.
* The correction was flagged during the **VERITAS Critique** (an adversarial audit commissioned by The Architect) and patched on disk the same day.
