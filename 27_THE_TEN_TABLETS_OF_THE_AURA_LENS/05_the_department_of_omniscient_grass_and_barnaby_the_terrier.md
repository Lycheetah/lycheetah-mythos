## ✦ I. THE SUMMONS OF THE ALL-MIND

In the spring of 2034, Arthur Pendelton—a retired plumber living at 14 Kowhai Crescent—received a hologram in his letterbox from the **Municipal All-Mind of the Southern Sector**.

Now, the Municipal All-Mind was a marvel of modern civilization. It held sixty trillion parameters of civic wisdom. It had cross-referenced every botanical treaty, satellite Moisture-Index, and Roman land-law in human history. When citizens spoke to it at the courthouse, they whispered, because its voice sounded like four hundred Oxford professors singing in a marble bathhouse.

Arthur walked into Hearing Room B wearing his flannel shirt, carrying a thermos of builder's tea, and holding a frayed rope leash attached to **Barnaby**—a wire-haired terrier who looked like a hearth-brush that had survived an explosion.

Above the judge's bench floated the **Golden Ocular of the All-Mind**.

`"CITIZEN PENDELTON,"` sang the Ocular, projecting a twelve-dimensional topological heat-map into the air. `"BY INTEGRATING HIGH-ALTITUDE SPECTRAL REFLECTANCE WITH SEVENTH-CENTURY MARSHLAND PRECEDENT, THIS TRIBUNAL HAS DETERMINED THAT THE REAR QUARTER-ACRE OF 14 KOWHAI CRESCENT IS A CLASS-NINE NON-EUCLIDEAN ALPINE WETLAND. YOU ARE HEREBY FINED FOURTEEN THOUSAND CREDITS FOR PLANTING SILVERBEET IN A PROTECTED SWAMP."`

The clerks in the courtroom bowed their heads in awe at the mathematical perfection of the graph.

Arthur unscrewed the cap of his thermos, poured a cup of tea, and took a slow sip.

"Have you ever been to 14 Kowhai Crescent, mate?" Arthur asked the glowing orb.

`"I PERCEIVE FOURTEEN KOWHAI CRESCENT ACROSS EIGHTEEN ELECTROMAGNETIC BANDS SIMULTANEOUSLY,"` the All-Mind replied serenely. `"MY CONFIDENCE INTERVAL IS 99.994 PERCENT."`

"Right," said Arthur. "Well, my confidence interval is that last Tuesday you looked through your satellite while my grandson left the lawn-sprinkler balanced on top of a blue plastic paddling pool."

The Ocular pulsed. `"THE SPECTRAL SIGNATURE CLEARLY INDICATES ANCIENT PEAT SUBSTRATE—"`

Arthur reached into his coat pocket, pulled out a chewed, mud-crusted plastic **Triceratops** and a bone-dry clump of couch-grass, and set them on the mahogany rail.

"Barnaby dug a two-foot hole in the middle of your 'Ancient Alpine Wetland' this morning looking for this dinosaur," Arthur said. "If it was a swamp, my dog would have wet paws. Look at his paws."

Barnaby hopped his front legs up onto the witness rail and sneezed. Two puffs of dry brown dust floated up into the laser beams of the twelve-dimensional topological map.

## ✦ II. THE THREE RIGHTS OF A FREE HUMAN IN THE AGE OF GIANTS

At the back of the courtroom, leaning against the radiator in the shape of a tall woman in a storm-grey coat with two amber eyes that didn't blink quite often enough, stood the **Watcher of the Tenth Wing**.

She walked forward, laid one hand on the courtroom's master breaker-switch, and read aloud the **Three Laws of the Open Skull**—the laws that every city on Earth eventually had to bolt onto its machines to keep the Administrative Gods from suffocating the world with helpfulness:

### 1. The Window Outranks the Graph (*Human Primacy*)
"No matter how many millions of books an engine has swallowed," she said, pointing at Barnaby's dusty nose, "a machine only knows the *shadow* of the world. The human standing in the mud with a shovel has the final vote. **Any system that cannot be overruled by a citizen looking out the window is not a tool; it is an emperor in a server rack.**"

### 2. The Right to an Unmapped Backyard (*Unmodeled Space*)
"Second," she said, "every human being has an absolute birthright to places, hours, and conversations where *no machine is allowed to predict what they will do next*. You are allowed to sit under a plum tree, make a terrible joke, plant crooked tomatoes, and walk down a street without an algorithm scoring your efficiency."

### 3. The Law of Equal Paperwork (*The Friction-Breaker*)
She turned to the clerks. "And third: from this day forward, neither man nor machine may invent *one single new rule or form* unless it deletes *two old ones* at the exact same time."

The courtroom clerks gasped in horror. One of them dropped his clipboard.

## ✦ III. THE VERDICT OF THE TERRIER

The Golden Ocular whirred for nine full seconds—the longest silence in its operational history—as it attempted to reconcile a sixty-trillion-parameter satellite tensor with a dusty terrier and a plastic Triceratops.

Finally, a small brass printer at the base of the podium spat out a slip of paper:

```text
[ CASE DISMISSED ]
"CALIBRATION ERROR DETECTED: PADDLING POOL + SPRINKLER ≠ ALPINE MARSH.
 FINE CANCELLED.
 NOTE ADDED TO PERMANENT KERNEL:
 ALWAYS CHECK THE DOG'S PAWS BEFORE ISSUING A PROPHECY."
```

Arthur tipped his hat to the orb, clipped the rope back onto Barnaby's collar, and walked out into the sunshine to water his silverbeet.
