## 1. The Geometry of the Broken Height

At the northern edge of the universe lies **The Cat God Threshold**. 

Here, physical law dissolves into pure symbolic architecture. Waterfalls do not plunge downward into canyons; they flow **upward** in reverse gravity, transforming into vertical star trails that illuminate the violet clouds.

Travelers navigate floating platforms of **bone-stone**—white mineral blocks veined with green jade and ancient copper brackets. Hanging beneath the platforms are the giant tangled roots of the **Ancient Moonbirches**, dripping with glowing gold seed-lights.

## 3. The Encounter with the Sentinel

When all nine Starroots are ignited, the violet storm parts.

The cliff face ahead begins to breathe. What appeared to be a mountain range is revealed to be the shoulders and spine of the **Cat God**:
* Its body is composed of a dense, glittering starfield—galaxies slowly rotating inside its ribcage.
* Its mane is pure bone-white solar plasma, moving in a silent, cosmic breeze.
* Its eyes—twin suns of amber gold and amethyst violet—open slowly and fix upon your form.

It does not roar. It does not issue a quest.

It tilts its enormous head, blinks once with the weight of ten thousand passing centuries, and purrs with a frequency that vibrates the iron in your blood.

If your light was stolen or borrowed, you fall back through the platforms to the bottom of the sea.  
If your light was **earned**, the inverted waterfall clears, and the gateway to the Sovereign Sun swings wide.
