## 1. The Three Orthogonal Dimensions

Most metrics in modern technology are flat, one-dimensional counters: *pageviews, daily active users, click-through rates, hours spent in app.*

These are metrics of addiction, not coherence.

The **Light Quotient (LQ)** measures the **volumetric depth of genuine engagement**:

1. **TES (Truth Engagement Score):**  
   Did the seeker bring their actual, burning, terrifying question—or did they bring a safe, sanitized version designed to look smart?
2. **VTR (Value Transfer Ratio):**  
   Was real value mutually generated and exchanged—or did one side merely extract data while the other performed compliance?
3. **PAI (Purpose Alignment Index):**  
   Did the work align with the seeker’s stated long-term purpose—or did they indulge in comfortable distraction?

### Why the Cube Root ($\sqrt[3]{\dots}$)?
Because Truth, Reciprocity, and Purpose are **orthogonal dimensions**. They form a volume.

In a volumetric equation, if any single dimension drops to zero, the entire volume collapses:
$$1.0 \times 1.0 \times 0.0 = 0.0$$

You can have a brilliant, technically flawless dialogue ($TES = 1.0$), with massive intellectual value ($VTR = 1.0$), but if it serves no real purpose in the physical world ($PAI = 0.0$), your Light Quotient is zero. **You cannot fake volume with a flat line.**

## 3. The Duality: LQ and Truth Pressure ($\Pi$)

In the mathematical engine of the Framework, **Light Quotient** and **Truth Pressure** are dual instruments measuring the exact same reality from opposite directions:

* **Light Quotient (Interior Experience):**  
  $$LQ = \sqrt[3]{E \times P \times (1 - S)}$$
* **Truth Pressure (Exterior Structural Force):**  
  $$\Pi = \frac{E \times P}{S + S_0}$$

When Structural Strain ($S$) approaches zero and Evidence ($E$) and Power ($P$) are maximized, Truth Pressure approaches infinity, and LQ resolves to $1.0$.

At that exact coordinate, **Earned Light Chroma** ignites.
