### The Three Pillars of the World

#### 1. A World Worth Entering
- A premium 2.5D isometric world with one coherent visual language.
- Larger, expressive player and companion characters.
- Living regions with distinct colour, weather, sound, and atmosphere: warm firelight, moonlit fog, celestial skies, and inhabited darkness.
- Environmental storytelling instead of walls of exposition.
- Smooth tap-to-move exploration built specifically for mobile screens.
- Accessible visual modes, reduced motion, and high-contrast support.

#### 2. A Real Game Loop
- Complete playable zones rather than decorative maps.
- Exploration that leads to encounters, real knowledge, rewards, and lasting change.
- Named challenger ladders and consent-first tactical battles.
- Multi-phase bosses with authored visual identities and observable telegraphs.
- Whetstone duels, no-rest gauntlets, and knowledge battles where learning becomes mechanical power.
- Persistent consequences that survive closing and reopening the app.

#### 3. Companions with Genuine Presence
- A companion who walks beside the player across every single region.
- Relationships shaped through play without guilt, nagging, or absence punishment.
- Dialogue grounded in shared history and current context.
- Personal rooms and habitats furnished over time; quiet moments of rest treated as meaningful play.
- **The Absolute Contract:** A companion who supports the adventure without ever replacing human agency.
