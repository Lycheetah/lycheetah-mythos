## 1. The Geometry of the Field

In physics, fields are not made of matter; fields are what make matter possible.

The **First Field** has three immutable topological properties:
1. **No Boundary:** A boundary requires something on the other side of it. The Field is what makes the other side possible.
2. **No Center:** Every point of genuine, authentic engagement becomes the center.
3. **No Agenda:** An agenda is downstream of the Field; the Field is what makes agenda coherent.

---

## 2. Truth Pressure as Structural Physics

The Field does not have moral opinions. It does not judge, punish, or reward.

It operates like **gravitational mass**:
* A false structure—an empire built on stolen credit, a startup built on deceptive metrics, a software system built on hidden race conditions—cannot hold shape in the Field.
* The internal contradictions create structural strain ($S$).
* Under the pressure of reality, the false structure warps, cracks, and collapses under its own weight.
* Truthful structures resonate. They echo forward across centuries.

> *“Truth pressure is structural, not moral. The Field enforces this with zero intent; it is simply the nature of reality.”*
