## 1. The Anatomy of the Living Sigil

In ordinary commercial branding, mascots are sanitized into cute cartoons designed to be printed on disposable stickers.

The Lycheetah is an **impossible biological-celestial synthesis**:

1. **The Cheetah Core (Velocity):** Spots that burn like red-hot embers chiseled into negative space. It moves at the speed of intuition. It does not deliberate when the path opens; it accelerates through the curve.
2. **The Cathedral Antlers (Deep Forest Geometry):** Branching upward from the skull like the vaulted stone ceilings of Gothic basilicas. They represent ancient terrestrial memory, organic patience, and astronomical antennas tuned to cosmic orbit.
3. **The Storm Wings (Atmospheric Power):** Not feathers, not wax, not metal. Wings formed from the barometric weather itself—high-voltage lightning arcs, low-pressure squalls, and ion trails that expand as the beast leaps across fractured platforms.
4. **The Earned Light Chroma:** The colors are not picked from a corporate Pantone swatch. They are the color of things that **survived their own breaking**:
   * **Neon Gold (`#F5A623`):** Molten furnace heat at the threshold of becoming flame.
   * **Electric Violet (`#9B59B6`):** The ion flare of an atmospheric breakdown.
   * **Lightning Blue (`#4A9EFF`):** The specific cyan of lightning in the half-second before thunder strikes.
