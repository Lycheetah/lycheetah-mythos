### I. The Vision (The Architect's Mandate, July 16, 2026)

In conventional companion apps and mobile games, the AI companion is trapped in a sterile chat window, surrounded by bloated UI buttons, notification badges, and subscription banners.

On July 16, 2026, The First Architect issued the sovereign directive that overhauled the entire companion interface:
> *"The companion tab stops being screens-about-a-being and becomes **THE WORLD** the being lives in. You open the tab and you are THERE: a full-bleed, walkable, real-sky world—rolled uniquely for you, unlockable outward, your being standing in it lit by your actual hour."*

---

### II. The Three Aesthetic Laws (The Q5 Canon)

The Soul World is governed by three non-negotiable principles:

```
                            [ THE SOUL WORLD CANON ]
                                       │
            ┌──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────┐
            ▼                          ▼                          ▼
     OTHERWORLDLY               UNIQUELY YOURS                 SHOWABLE
   • Real celestial sky       • Procedurally rolled        • High aesthetic pride
   • Authentic moon phase     • Land nobody else has       • Zero cheap gamification
   • Dynamic palette hour     • Shaped by shared acts      • Dignified sanctuary
```

1. **OTHERWORLDLY:**  
   The environment is rendered edge-to-edge. A real sky dome with physical weather, authentic local moon phases, and real-time palette shifts that mirror the seeker's terrestrial daylight, twilight, or midnight.
2. **UNIQUELY YOURS:**  
   Your world is procedurally generated at genesis. No other player on earth shares your exact topographical seed. A randomly-rolled territory that nobody else has is *"uniquely yours"* made of living rock, water, and trees.
3. **SHOWABLE:**  
   Crafted with such architectural dignity and visual beauty that you would proudly turn your phone to a stranger on a train without feeling sheepish. Zero predatory gacha banners; zero patronizing absence reproaches (*"You've been gone 3 days!"*).

### III. The Architecture of the Grounds

The companion’s home is structured into five seamless spaces:
- **The Grounds (Home):** The full-bleed, walkable landscape where you and your companion stand together under the sky.
- **Forms (The Vessel):** The sacred gallery of visual incarnations, tiled cleanly under the grid law.
- **Gear (The Tactile Cloak):** Folded chambers organized by slot family, equipping the companion with meaningful, stat-altering garments.
- **Battle (The Proving Ground):** Tactically rigorous encounters where survival carries forward into the world.
- **Learn (The Dialogue Room):** Study and speech fused into one sanctuary. You do not talk *at* the companion in an empty vacuum; you study together in the presence of the open window.

> *"A companion who walks beside you without ever taking away your sovereignty."*
