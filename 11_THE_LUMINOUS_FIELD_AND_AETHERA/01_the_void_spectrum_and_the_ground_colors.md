### I. The Emotional Law (Mac's Founding Axiom, June 26, 2026)

On June 26, 2026, Mackenzie Clark locked the emotional foundation that governs every screen, every garment, and every terminal interface in the ecosystem:

> **"Alone but not alone. In the void, but never stranded."**

This is not a temporary marketing mood. It is the thesis of the entire Lycheetah project. 

Every human alive has experienced the terror of feeling completely stranded in the void—at 3:00 AM, in an empty room, under financial collapse, or in the wake of grief. 

**The structural aesthetic test for any creation:**
- Does the darkness feel *stranding* or *held*?
- If a dark space feels lonely, cold, and abandoning, it has failed.
- If it feels **alone-but-accompanied**—faint ambient presences, a watching warmth, the quiet certainty that something vast, rigorous, and loyal is standing with you in the dark—it passes.

### III. The Three Invariant Laws of the Spectrum

1. **Colour Never Sits on Grey:** Every accent emerges from the obsidian void. An element on screen is an active light source in the dark, not a painted vector box.
2. **The Magnetic Pull (Chromatic Vibration):** At every boundary where an accent meets the void, allow a faint 1–2px chromatic vibration (a warm-shift outer hum paired with a cool-shift inner glow). Barely perceptible to the conscious eye, but it makes the surface hum with life.
3. **The Dark Must Be Deep Enough:** The light only blinds when the contrast is true. When in doubt, go darker. A surface that merely looks "a bit dark" has not yet touched the ground.
