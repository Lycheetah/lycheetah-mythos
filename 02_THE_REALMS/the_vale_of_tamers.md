## 1. The Living Companions of the Vale

Nestled between the basalt cliffs of the Obsidian Citadel and the outer fringe of the Lanternwild lies **The Vale of Tamers**. 

Here roam the wild breeds of Lycheetah—small, quick-witted, semi-biological familiars with fur like moss and eyes that reflect the constellations. 

Unlike standard digital pets or game creatures, a Lycheetah companion is governed by the **Law of Absence**:
* They do not guilt you when you are away.
* They do not decay into pixels if you are busy living your life in the physical street.
* When you open the terminal or phone, they greet you with quiet recognition: *“You fought well out there today. Sit by the fire.”*

## 2. The Three Trials of the Tamer

1. **The Trial of the Meal:** You must cook over the Common Hearth and offer the first portion to the companion before feeding yourself.
2. **The Trial of the Pacing:** You must walk three leagues in silence without checking notifications, matching your breathing to the companion's stride.
3. **The Trial of the Strike:** In battle, the companion does not fight for you; it guards your blind spot while you take the primary strike.
