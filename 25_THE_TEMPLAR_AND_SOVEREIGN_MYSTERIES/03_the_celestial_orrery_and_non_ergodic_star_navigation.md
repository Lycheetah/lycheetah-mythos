## ✦ PART III · THE HIGH MYSTERY: THE ERGODIC FALLACY PROOF
*(The Mathematical Resolution of Dispute 700)*

Why does unsupervised multi-agent consensus inevitably decay into hallucination or ruin over long horizons? The initiate proves it via **Peters's Non-Ergodic Dynamics** applied to epistemic trust capital $X_i(t)$:

### 1. The Multiplicative Epistemic Process
When an agent seat chains reasoning steps sequentially across time $t$, errors compound multiplicatively under geometric Brownian motion with drift $\mu$ and volatility $\sigma$:

$$dX_i(t) = \mu X_i(t)\,dt + \sigma X_i(t)\,dW_i(t)$$

### 2. The Divergence of Ensemble vs. Time Average
* **The Ensemble Average (What Benchmark Papers Measure):** Across $N \to \infty$ parallel trials at a fixed snapshot, expectation grows exponentially:
  $$\mathbb{E}_{\text{ensemble}}[X(t)] = X(0)\,e^{\mu t} \to +\infty \quad (\text{for } \mu > 0)$$
* **The Time Average (What a Real Project Experiences):** By Itô's Lemma, the trajectory of an actual running system converges almost surely to the time-average growth rate $g_{\text{time}}$:
  $$g_{\text{time}} = \lim_{t \to \infty} \frac{1}{t} \ln X_i(t) = \mu - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 \quad \text{a.s.}$$

Whenever variance exceeds twice the drift ($\frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 > \mu > 0$), **the ensemble average explodes to $+\infty$ while every individual unanchored swarm trajectory collapses almost surely to $0$ ($X_i(t) \to 0$).**

### 3. The Covenantal Anchor (Volatility Truncation)
How does the Lycheetah Orrery break the non-ergodic ruin trap?
