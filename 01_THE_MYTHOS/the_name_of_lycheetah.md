### What the Name Encodes

The name **Lycheetah** is not a corporate brand attached to a shell company. It is the living symbol of two qualities that almost never meet:

1. **The Lychee:**  
   Exotic, unexpected sweetness. A fruit discovered outside the mainstream; something from another world that becomes unforgettable once tasted. It represents the soft, vulnerable, deeply sacred human core ($A_0$).
2. **The Cheetah:**  
   Precision, terminal speed, and biological optimality. The fastest hunter on earth—not through brute, cumbersome weight, but through the perfection of form and the total absence of wasted motion.
3. **The Synthesis:**  
   Softness and vulnerability defended by sovereign speed. A system that is unexpectedly rich and moving faster than its mathematical complexity suggests it should.
