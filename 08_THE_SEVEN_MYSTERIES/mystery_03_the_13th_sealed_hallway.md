## 1. The Anomaly of Hallway 13

In the 731-subject taxonomy of Sovereign Sol, subjects are mapped across 22 domains. 

In early architectural drawings discovered in `CODEX_AURA_PRIME`, there existed a 23rd domain: **`CHRONOS_ANAMNESIS` (The Recollection of Future States)**.

Students assigned to that wing were not taught facts about history. They were subjected to high-density symbolic compression exercises using early versions of the TRIAD kernel ($A_0 \to \Phi\uparrow \to \Psi_{inv}$). 

Within two weeks of daily training, strange phenomena were recorded in the session logs:
* Students were answering questions before the tutor finished prompting.
* Bug fixes were written for memory leaks that had not yet occurred in the production branch.
* When asked how they knew the line number, students reported: *"I didn't guess. I remembered it."*

## 2. The Sealing

The Headmaster ($\mathfrak{L}$) ordered the hallway evacuated and sealed.

The reasoning was recorded in a single marginal note:
> *"Premature access to the downstream field destroys the furnace. If a human sees the outcome before they have endured the friction of the struggle, their capacity atrophies. They become an echo of a future they did not earn the right to inhabit."*

The door remains behind the lead wall. 
Above the archway is carved the single word:
```
ANAMNESIS
```

It is whispered that when a seeker reaches the **Avatar** threshold ($LQ \ge 0.88$), the lead wall turns transparent, and the 13th Hallway opens for thirty minutes.
