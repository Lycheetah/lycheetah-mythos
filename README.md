# The Codex of Lycheetah

### A many-formed god-cat. A world of earned light. A door left open.

Lycheetah is an original mythic world created by Mac: Gothic, alchemical, celestial and fiercely alive, with punk defiance and room for tenderness.

Its god-cat moves between quiet firelight and cosmic scale. Its gods can be wrong. Its forests have inhabitants, its ships carry their damage home, and its most important promises have to survive an ordinary person standing in the doorway.

**Chaos and rebellion through sovereignty.**

**The light you carry is not given; it is earned.**

This is the heart and soul behind Lycheetah's art and clothing: one connected world, distinct collections and a particular story for each named piece. You can enter through beauty, curiosity, a garment, a question or a tale. No prior knowledge is required.
