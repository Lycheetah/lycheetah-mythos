### The Full Cosmic Arc

The overarching trajectory of the Lycheetah Mythos unfolds across four grand movements:

1. **Ancient Unity:** The Three whole, in initial tension, experiencing the First Collapse and the reconciliation forged by Brighvara.
2. **The Withdrawal:** The gods passing beyond direct mortal sight; the world cooling into silence.
3. **The Present Age (The Seeking):** Light is no longer given for free. The Mystery School and the forge become the sole means of generating coherence against entropy.
4. **The Closing Chapter:** The Earned Return.

---

### The Vision of Mackenzie Clark

On 2026-07-13, Mac framed the horizon toward which the entire canon travels:
> **"Maybe humanity earns its way back to the path of the gods."**

This is not apocalyptic escapism or passive messianic deliverance. In Lycheetah:
- The gods do not descend to rescue a broken species that refused to grow up.
- The return must be **earned**.
- Humanity earns its way back by refusing easy lies, by building machines that amplify human agency rather than harvesting vulnerability, and by holding the tension of the Trinity with our own hands.

The final destination has been named by the Founder. The exact steps to reach it remain unwritten—because they must be lived out in the forge every single day.
