## ✦ I. WHAT THIS EPIC IS

*The Book of the Long Light* is the character-led epic translation of the Lycheetah universe.

The existing Twelve Books of the Field are compressed scripture and compiler logic: the Field, the Athanor, Sol, CASCADE, AURA, LAMAGUE, the Mystery School, Earned Light, the Four Teachers, Field Echoes, the Seekers, and the Sovereign. *The Book of the Long Light* does not replace them. It makes their world inhabitable through people, creatures, danger, discovery, humour, grief, puzzles, companionship, and consequential choice.

### The Plain Door and the Deeper Room
Every narrative movement operates on two simultaneous frequencies:
1. **The Plain Door (Story):** A living human journey through physical terrain—Mara, Tarin, Ember, Veyra, the Waybearer, the silent Cat-God sentinel, the red wastes, and the nine alabaster towers.
2. **The Deeper Room (Framework):** The formal architecture of the Lycheetah Framework operating as the underlying metaphysical physics of the cosmos. When an archway turns into an institutional classification, it is the failure of the AURA invariants. When a path is walked against the grain, integrity debt accumulates.

---

## ✦ II. THE INDEPENDENCE LAW

1. **Not a Disguised Biography:** This is not Mackenzie Conor James Clark's personal life disguised as fantasy. His lived experience remains the emotional and philosophical furnace, but personal relationships, private movements, and disclosed hardships are never plot material. The story stands fully on its own feet.
2. **Transmutation of Truth:** Universal truth is admitted only after it has passed through the alchemical crucible into original characters, cultures, and consequences.
3. **No Decorative Filler:** No arbitrary magic systems, no unearned prophecies, no chosen-one shortcuts. Every miracle is paid for in thermodynamic energy and earned light.

## ✦ III. THE CORE CHARACTERS OF THE FIRST CYCLE

* **Mara:** An archivist and repairer at the edge of the South Archive. Pragmatic, stubborn, armed with an archival brass ruler and an instinctive refusal to let living things be converted into museum specimens.
* **Tarin:** A stone-mason and field repairer with ash on his sleeves, an endless collection of iron wedges, half a slice of bread between his teeth, and a quiet loyalty that asks no questions when the walls begin to fall.
* **Veyra:** The austere guardian and instructor whose presence anchors the threshold of the Mystery School. When the dead bell rings, the word burning across her chest is `WARDEN`—the tension between protection and imprisonment.
* **The Waybearer:** A horned celestial beast bonded to Mara through the Bind. Silenced brass bells hanging from its harness; communication occurring not through voice, but through directional pressure that aches in the bone.
* **The Celestial Beast (Lycheetah):** The feline shadow that haunts the firelight of the First Field. Antlers branching like primeval trees, weather gathering as storm wings at its shoulders, spots burning like coals through night.
