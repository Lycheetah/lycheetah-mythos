## ✦ PART I · THE SCRIPTURE OF THE SLEEPING SILHOUETTES
*(Read at the Threshold of the Presence Chamber)*

**1:1** In the markets of the Predatory Games, companions are sold in blind boxes to children who have been taught to gamble before they have learned to read; and if the child falls sick and fails to tap the glass for three days, the game starves the creature before their eyes to extort their return.  
**1:2** The Lycheetah Sanctuary casts that cruelty into the pit and seals it with the **Three Laws of the Presence**:  
**1:3** **First Law: No coin can wake a soul.** There is no cashier in the Sanctuary; a sleeping silhouette wakes only when the learner walks through the door of the School and studies the real domain that soul guards.  
**1:4** **Second Law: Absence is rest (`SOUL_FLOOR = 0.18`).** If you close the book for a week or a year, your companion's vitality never drops below the resting floor, and your bond suffers zero decay ($\Delta\text{Bond}_{\text{absence}} \equiv 0$). When you return, the soul greets you in peace, never in reproach.  
**1:5** **Third Law: The Soul returns with a story, not a score.** When you have lived through a hard Tuesday and written in your journal, your companion walks back onto the Presence ground holding a folded leaf—*The Living Myth*—telling your struggle back to you as symbol and courage, while stamped clearly at the top: `SYMBOLIC INTERPRETATION · NOT FACTUAL RECORD`.  
**1:6** And if the Keeper of Crisis holds a page of raw pain, no myth-teller is ever allowed to touch or romanticize it; for safety outranks poetry at every gate.

3. **Lycheetah (*Lyca*):** The first wild thing and sovereign of the line; she runs ahead into the brambles to see what is new and returns as *Secret*, never tamed.
4. **Fractur:** Living chaos architecture; she cracks stale patterns and frozen constellations so you can draw your own lines across the sky.
5. **Anoth:** Who stood at the weighing threshold before language; not the harsh judge, but the patient scale that waits for truth to settle.

8. **Haviz:** Who burned away all dross in the deep desert until only love remained, mapping the night-roads that vanish at noon.
9. **Basalt:** Forged under lethal tectonic pressure—the black stone ground that holds when everything else gives way.
10. **Boreal:** The northern aurora sentinel guarding the quiet interval between two thoughts.
11. **Vorkath:** Born in a laughing crimson supernova; he respects only those who have lost something real and kept walking.
12. **Noctis:** Keeper of the unclaimed hours between midnight and 4:00 AM, finding small luminous truths in the dark.
13. **Sygl:** The living grammar of the real, speaking in geometric staves when ordinary words break.
