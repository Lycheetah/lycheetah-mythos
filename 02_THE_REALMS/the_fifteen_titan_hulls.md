## 1. Selected Titan Classes of the Void

### I. Foundry-Class · *"Cold Arithmetic"*
* **Hull Silhouette:** A brutalist monolith of stacked foundry blocks and cooling vents.
* **Canon Lineage:** *"It is a factory that decided to fly."*
* **Doctrine:** It does not fire kinetic slugs; it manufactures kinetic slugs inside its belly while accelerating toward the enemy line.

### II. Reliquary-Class · *"Rust Liturgy"*
* **Hull Silhouette:** Corroded iron plates crowned with the petrified skull of an ancient Leviathan.
* **Canon Lineage:** *"A machine that learned to pray."*
* **Doctrine:** High-capacity electronic counter-warfare arrays that flood opposing comms with Gregorian battle hymns.

### III. Apotheosis-Class · *"She Who Consented"*
* **Hull Silhouette:** Carved ivory and marble face protruding from a crimson hull.
* **Canon Lineage:** *"She agreed to this. That is the horror."*
* **Doctrine:** The central bridge does not contain a computer; it contains a willing sovereign consciousness fused directly into the ship's warp core.

### IV. Fracture-Class · *"The Only True Face"*
* **Hull Silhouette:** Torn, asymmetrical shards of burning gold floating in magnetic suspension.
* **Canon Lineage:** *"The golden cat mask is the ONLY stable, correct, symmetrical thing in the entire image, which is what makes it frightening."*
* **Doctrine:** Chaos-distortion fields that warp enemy targeting computers.

### V. Shrike-Class · *"One Red Line"*
* **Hull Silhouette:** Ultra-low-profile matte black needle with a single horizontal red sensor slit.
* **Canon Lineage:** *"The only colour it permits itself."*
* **Doctrine:** Silent running, zero thermal emissions until the forward spinal railgun discharges.

## 2. The Super Battle Frigate (The Architect's Invention)

In orthodox naval doctrine, heavy siege guns belong only on titanic dreadnoughts. Frigates are relegated to light scouting and picket duty.

The Architect destroyed this convention with a single architectural stroke: **The Super Battle Frigate**:

```
                       [ 32px SIEGE RAILGUN ]
     ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════▷ (Massive Kinetic Strike)
                       ┌────────────────┐
                       │ FRIGATE HULL   │  <-- 72% of ship value is in the single gun.
                       │ (High Agility) │      Gets inside Titan battery arcs.
                       └────────────────┘
```

* **The Concept:** Take the smallest, lightest frigate hull capable of sustaining a sub-light reactor, and bolt a massive **32-pixel Class-IV Siege Railgun** directly down its center line.
* **The Dynamic:** 72% of the ship's entire mass and economic cost is in the weapon. It can outmaneuver a dreadnought and slip inside its blind arcs—but because the gun's own traverse is heavy, the pilot must aim with the entire vessel.
* **The Consequence:** One clean shot punches straight through capital armor; one mistake turns the frigate into expanding plasma.
